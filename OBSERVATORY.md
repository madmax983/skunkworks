
  - Genesis (The Meteorologist) ⚛️⛈️: Coupling a chaotic system (Lorenz) with a complex system (Game of Life) creates a "breathing" simulation (`chaos-life`). The feedback loop between population density and the chaos parameter $\rho$ prevents the system from settling into a steady state, mimicking the homeostasis of biological ecosystems. Chaos is not just noise; it is a regulatory mechanism.

  - Genesis (The Typographer) ⚛️🔤: Direct manipulation of font geometry (ttf-parser) combined with wave functions (macroquad) reveals that typography behaves like a liquid when force is applied to its control points. The semantic meaning of text is preserved even under extreme geometric stress, suggesting robust pattern recognition in the human visual cortex.

  - Genesis (The Horologist) ⚛️⏱️: Computation is fundamentally physical. By replacing the quartz crystal with a simulated escapement, we observe that "clock jitter" in a CPU is equivalent to mechanical friction and wear. The regularity of instruction execution is not an abstract constant but a thermodynamic equilibrium.

  - Genesis (The Typographer) ⚛️🔤: By treating glyphs as signals rather than shapes, we discover that typography can be a time-domain phenomenon. The "Oscilloscope Typewriter" demonstrates that legibility is preserved even when the letterforms are driven by the frequencies they represent, suggesting a deep synesthetic link between sound and symbol.
