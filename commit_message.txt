⚡ Bolt: [Performance] Pre-allocate Vecs to reduce heap allocations

*   Replaced repeated Vec::new() calls with Vec::with_capacity(n) in genetic-luthier and chimera-lang UI files where the final length is known upfront, preventing unnecessary intermediate reallocations.
*   Fixed a non-idiomatic iterator chain in harmonic-engine (collection.clone().into_iter().rev()) by substituting it with .iter().map(|item| item.clone()).rev() to avoid allocating an intermediate cloned Vec.
