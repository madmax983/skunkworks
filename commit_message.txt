⚡ Bolt: Replace `.extend(collection.clone())` with `.extend(collection.iter().cloned())`

💡 What: Replaced `.extend(collection.clone())` with `.extend(collection.iter().cloned())` in several files within `chimera-lang`.
🎯 Why: To avoid unnecessary intermediate heap allocations. Calling `clone()` on a collection allocates a new vector on the heap before extending the target vector. `iter().cloned()` avoids this by chaining an iterator.
📊 Impact: Eliminates unnecessary `Vec` allocations during gene collection operations, such as in genetics processing, alchemy fusion, and acoustic compilation.
🔬 Measurement: Run `cargo clippy` and test suite `cargo test -p chimera-lang` to verify safety and correctness.
