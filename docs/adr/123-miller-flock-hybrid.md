# 123. Miller-Flock Hybrid

Date: 2026-06-18

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Splice Surgeon created a new experimental hybrid called `miller-flock`, exploring swarm intelligence navigating structural codebase typography. This experiment crosses the rigid 3D crystalline lattice generated by filesystem structures (`crates/miller-lattice`) with the continuous emergent herding behavior of boids (`crates/flocking`).

## Decision

We map the structural crystal nodes of a codebase (the Miller lattice) into spatial attractors for emergent swarm intelligence. The 3D crystalline lattice is flattened into a 2D topographical space. The discrete crystal nodes act as intense spatial attractors that emergent swarm behavior (boids) naturally navigates and clusters around.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Enables an emergent biological visualization of codebase hot-spots, where swarms of boids navigate the topography of a repository, naturally clustering and foraging around densely coupled structural nodes while avoiding sparse voids.
- **Negative:** The hybrid must continuously recalculate attractive forces from potentially thousands of stationary "structural nodes" towards thousands of moving "boids", scaling quadratically without advanced spatial partitioning.
