Date: 2026-07-11

## Status

Proposed

## Context

The Splice Surgeon created a new experimental hybrid called `gray-poincare` (Hyperbolic Morphogenesis). This cross explores mapping by crossing the continuous, thermodynamic scalar field of `gray-scott` with the continuous non-Euclidean geometry of `poincare-disk`.

## Decision

Cross the `gray-scott` reaction-diffusion with `poincare-disk`. Maps the growing Turing patterns (reaction-diffusion blobs) into the hyperbolic space constraints, stretching and compressing them as they drift towards the boundary.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** Produces an Escher-like biological simulation where flat chemical concentration blobs undergo physical non-Euclidean warping as they spread.
