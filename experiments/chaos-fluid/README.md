# Chaos Fluid

**Lineage:**
- 🧬 Parent A: `experiments/chaos-pendulum` (Chaotic macro-physics)

**Phenotype Prediction & Result:**
*Macroscopic Chaos vs Microscopic Fluidics*. The chaotic double pendulum's bobs act as strong moving magnetic poles within a ferrous fluid simulation. As the chaotic pendulum dynamically stirs and repels the magnetic fluid, it creates swirling, unpredictable fluid dynamic patterns mapped directly to the strange attractor of the pendulum.

## Novel Traits
- The pendulum's nodes are mapped as highly charged magnetic dipoles.
- The `Universe` calculates physical advection driven by the nodes' movement over the `Platter`.
- A mesmerizing juxtaposition between the discrete mathematical complexity of chaos theory and the continuous physical nature of fluid dynamics.
