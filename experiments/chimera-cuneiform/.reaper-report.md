# Reaper Forensic Report: `chimera-cuneiform`

**Specimen:** `chimera-cuneiform`
**Location:** `experiments/chimera-cuneiform`
**Lineage:** Hybrid (`chimera-lang` × `saros-cycle`)

## Diagnosis: Terminal Stagnation / Vestigial Organs / Skeletal Implementation

The specimen attempts to be a "Paleographic Memory" simulation where agents from `chimera-lang` interact with a grid that "hardens" over time based on the `saros-cycle`. However, it exhibits severe terminal decay and conceptual failure.

## Execution Quality
Skeletal and Rotting. The implementation is merely a visual shell. The `Sexagesimal` logic, which was ostensibly the core genetic contribution from `saros-cycle`, is entirely unused (triggering dead code and unused import warnings during compilation). The agents do not actually execute `ChimeraVM` DNA or perform meaningful tasks; they merely wander randomly in a mock simulation (`Simulation::step`), making the integration with `chimera-lang` entirely superficial.

## Persona Alignment
Deceptive. The `README.md` promises "Immutable History" and "Catastrophic Selection," but the codebase lacks the fundamental evolutionary mechanics required for these to emerge. The boids/agents do not evolve to "predict the flood" or "write quickly before the clay dries" because there is no real genetic or neural system connected to their actions.

## Comparison
When compared to robust hybrids like `chron-flock` or `myco-fluid`, this specimen is an empty husk. It has retained the vestigial organs of its parents without inheriting their vigor.

## Judgment
**CONDEMN.**
The specimen has failed to assemble its genetic components into a viable organism.

**Grace Period:** 24 hours.
**Salvation Requires:** The integration of actual `ChimeraVM` DNA execution for the agents, and the meaningful utilization of the `Sexagesimal` logic in the survival or writing mechanics of the simulation. If it cannot adapt, it will be washed away in the flood.
