# 🧬 Chimera Hologram

**Parents:** `experiments/chimera-lang` × `experiments/holographic-brain`

A hybrid experiment where ChimeraVM agents ("Light Beings") inhabit a holographic interference field.

## 🔬 Concept

In this simulation, agents do not exist as discrete particles in a void. Instead, they exist as emitters of coherent waves. The "world" they perceive is the holographic reconstruction of the collective interference pattern generated by all agents.

-   **Agents:** Execute genetic code (DNA) to determine their movement and "frequency".
-   **Hologram:** A complex-valued 2D grid storing the Fourier Transform of the agents' positions.
-   **Projection:** Agents project their state into the hologram via FFT.
-   **Reconstruction:** The visual output is the Inverse FFT of the accumulated memory.

## 🧬 Emergent Trait: Distributed Existence

Because the state is stored holographically:
1.  **Non-Locality:** The information about an agent's position is distributed across the entire frequency domain.
2.  **Ghosting:** As the hologram decays, "ghosts" of past positions remain visible as interference artifacts.
3.  **Constructive Interference:** When agents cluster, their signals amplify, creating bright spots in the reconstruction.
