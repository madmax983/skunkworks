# Chimera Strings

**Lineage:** `chimera-lang` (Genetic Virtual Machine) × `ferrous-strings` (Acoustic-Magnetic Symbiosis)

## Concept

Acoustic Genetic Symbiosis. `ChimeraVM` agents inhabit a continuous space filled with vibrating magnetic strings.

## Traits

-   **Magnetic Sensorimotor Control:** The agents navigate based on their genetic instructions, executing opcodes that read local magnetic fields emitted by the strings.
-   **Acoustic Foraging:** The agents pluck strings that they come in contact with.
-   **Genetic Evolution:** Agents evolve to cluster around specific resonant frequencies or navigate dense magnetic regions.

## Controls

-   **Space:** Clear agents.
-   **Click:** Pluck strings.
