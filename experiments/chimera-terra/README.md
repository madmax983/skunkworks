# Chimera Terra 🧬🌍

*A living landscape where the terrain is a brain and the inhabitants are code.*

## 🧬 Lineage

**Cross:** `turing-terra` × `chimera-lang`

- **Parent A (Structure):** `turing-terra` provided the Reaction-Diffusion (Gray-Scott) fluid simulation and the 3D terrain rendering infrastructure using `macroquad`.
- **Parent B (Life):** `chimera-lang` provided the `ChimeraVM`, the genetic code (DNA), and the evolutionary logic.

## ⚗️ Concept

In **Chimera Terra**, the landscape itself is a chemical computer (Reaction-Diffusion). Organisms, driven by `ChimeraVM` genetic code, inhabit this world.

- **Sensors:** Organisms sense the local chemical concentration (V) of the terrain. This input is fed into their neural/memory grid.
- **Actuators:** Organisms "think" using their DNA (OpCodes) and decide to Move, Eat, or Secrete.
- **Terraforming:**
  - **Eating:** Removes chemicals from the terrain, lowering mountains.
  - **Secreting:** Adds chemicals to the terrain, raising mountains.

The result is a feedback loop: The terrain shapes the life (via resource distribution), and the life shapes the terrain (via metabolism).

## 🎮 Controls

- **WASD**: Move Camera
- **Arrows**: Rotate Camera
- **Q/E**: Move Camera Up/Down
- **Space/Click**: Paint Catalyst (Add Chemical Blob)
- **R**: Reset Simulation and Repopulate
- **J/K**: Adjust Feed Rate
- **U/I**: Adjust Kill Rate

## 🧪 Emergent Behavior

Watch as the organisms cluster around high-energy zones (mountains/coral reefs) and graze them down, or seed new structures by secreting chemicals.
