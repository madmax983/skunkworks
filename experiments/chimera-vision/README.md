# Chimera Vision 🧬👁️

> "The Gaze of the Observer shapes the Evolution of the Observed."

**Chimera Vision** is a hybrid experiment combining `chimera-lang` (Genetic Programming) and `gaze-attractor` (Chaos Physics + Gaze Tracking).

It simulates a population of **ChimeraVM** agents inhabiting a "Chaos Field" (a dynamic physics mesh). The user acts as the "Observer" (or God), creating a "Fovea" of stability where they look.

## The Mechanic

*   **The World**: A mesh of connected nodes simulating a physical fabric. Velocity = Chaos/Energy.
*   **The Agents**: Living code (ChimeraVMs) that consume Energy to survive and execute DNA.
*   **The Gaze**:
    *   **Fovea (Where you look)**:
        *   Physics is dampened (Stable).
        *   Energy is abundant (Photosynthesis).
        *   Mutation is LOW (Stasis).
    *   **Periphery (Where you ignore)**:
        *   Physics is chaotic (High velocity/temperature).
        *   Energy drains rapidly (Entropy).
        *   Mutation is HIGH (Evolutionary acceleration).

## Controls

*   **Mouse**: Move the Gaze (Yellow Box).
*   **`m`**: Toggle Gaze Mode.
    *   **Stabilize (Cyan)**: Calms the physics. Standard mode.
    *   **Excite (Magenta)**: Agitates the physics. "Wrath of God" mode.
*   **`q`**: Quit.

## Lineage

*   **Parent A**: `experiments/chimera-lang` (Genetics, VM, Metabolism)
*   **Parent B**: `experiments/gaze-attractor` (Physics Engine, Gaze Logic)
*   **Novel Trait**: **Interactive Evolutionary Guidance**. The user shears the evolutionary landscape by simply looking at it.

## Phenotype Prediction

Agents should evolve to "surf" the edge of the gaze—staying close enough for safety/energy, but dipping into the chaos for occasional beneficial mutations. Alternatively, "extremophile" strains might evolve to survive purely in the high-entropy periphery.
