inside a journey leg — or by +750, past one. This planting keeps
the evidence room's packets, re-judgement, aversion and acquittal
rule verbatim, books caution by source exactly as
`wide-stagger-moths` does, expires **both** pools at lifetime
**750**, and sets the one offset to **+450 ticks** — the
claim-step itself, the mean span a moth spends travelling to a
claim in this cave — in three rooms:

  earned units at gain + 750.

  taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, at the step.
- **Coincident-step (the control)** — *both* pools expire at
  gain + 750 + 450. The deadlines stay coincident with each
  other; only the duration is longer, and the longest deadline
  (1950) sits between the +375 control's (1875) and the wide
  control's (2250).
