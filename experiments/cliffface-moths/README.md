## The Novel Trait

Every staggered room so far offset a deadline by +375 ticks —
inside a journey leg — by +450, at the claim-step itself, by
+600, at the shelf's edge, or by +750, past one. This planting
keeps the evidence room's packets, re-judgement, aversion and
acquittal rule verbatim, books caution by source exactly as
`shelfedge-moths` does, expires **both** pools at lifetime
**750**, and sets the one offset to **+525 ticks** — midway
between the claim-step and the edge, standing on the cliff's
face — in three rooms:

- **Staggered-face** — taught units expire at gain + 750 + 525;
  earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored-face** — earned units expire at gain + 750 + 525;
  taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, at the face.
- **Coincident-face (the control)** — *both* pools expire at
  gain + 750 + 525. The deadlines stay coincident with each
  other; only the duration is longer, and the longest deadline
  (2025) sits between the step control's (1950) and the edge
  control's (2100).

Thirty-two twin rooms, same seed: the twenty-nine parents,
unchanged (tests pin their ledgers exactly — including
staggered-step fed 50 / burns 9, mirrored-step fed 46 / burns
11, coincident-step fed 45 / burns 13, staggered-edge fed 56 /

## Falsification condition

This planting is wrong if, on another seed or cave, the face
rooms separate from these readings (staggered-face ≠ 16 or
mirrored-face ≠ 11 or coincident-face ≠ 16 here) — that would
make the early face a property of this seed's journeys, not
of the +525 address — or if a room at +475 or +500 already
reads an intermediate price (10..=15), which would give the
cliff a slope this planting says it does not have, or if a
room at +475 still reads 16 *and* a room at +450 on a finer
instrument reads above 9, which would move the face inside
the claim-step itself and make the shelf narrower than its
own name.
