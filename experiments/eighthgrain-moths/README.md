## The Novel Trait

opposite of halving: it prices the split's **neighbours**. The
evidence room's packets, re-judgement, aversion and acquittal
rule are kept verbatim, caution is booked by source exactly as
`quartergrain-moths` does, both pools expire at lifetime **750**,
and the one offset is set to **+469** (one west of the split's
western neighbour), **+470** (the western neighbour itself),
**+472** (the eastern neighbour), or **+473** (one east of it,
two west of the tread's first address at +475) — in three rooms
at each address:

- **Staggered-469 / -470 / -472 / -473** — taught units expire
  at gain + 750 + offset; earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored-469 / -470 / -472 / -473** — earned units expire
  at gain + 750 + offset; taught units at gain + 750. The
  mirror, at the eighth grain.
- **Coincident-469 / -470 / -472 / -473 (the controls)** —
  *both* pools expire at gain + 750 + offset. The deadlines
  stay coincident with each other; only the duration is longer
  (longest deadlines 1969 / 1970 / 1972 / 1973).

Seventy-seven twin rooms, same seed: the sixty-five parents,
unchanged (tests pin their ledgers exactly — including
staggered-471 fed 50 / burns 10, the split itself), plus the

## Falsification condition

eighth rooms separate from these readings (staggered-469
≠ 9 or staggered-470 ≠ 9 or staggered-472 ≠ 14 or
staggered-473 ≠ 14 or mirrored ≠ 11 or coincident ≠ 14 at

of this seed's journeys, not of the address — or if a room
at +471 on another seed reads 9 or 14 entire, which would
un-split the landing quartergrain reported and move the
taught first step whole into a single edge after all.
