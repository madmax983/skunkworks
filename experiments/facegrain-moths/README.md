## Falsification condition

grain rooms separate from these readings (staggered-475 ≠ 14
or coincident-475 ≠ 14 or staggered-500 ≠ 16 or
mirrored ≠ 11 at either grain here) — that would make the
landing a property of this seed's journeys, not of the +475
address — or if a room at +462 or +487 reads the shelf's 9
or the bottom's 16 respectively *and* a room between them

stair a slope this planting says it is not, or if a room at
+475 on a finer instrument finds the taught and coincident
lines parted there, which would split the one face this
planting reports into two.
