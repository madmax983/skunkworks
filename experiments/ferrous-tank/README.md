# 🧬 Ferrous Tank

**Status:** STABLE TRAIL
**Genotype:** `experiments/ferrous-fluid` × `experiments/ripple-tank`
**Phenotype:** Magnetic Acoustic Resonance

## Lineage Documentation

This experiment is a hybrid created by The Splice Surgeon. It combines the continuous, wave-propagating acoustic pressure field from `ripple-tank` with the discrete, magnetically-influenced particle physics of `ferrous-fluid`.

### From `ferrous-fluid` (Parent A)
- **Discrete Particles (`Particle`):** The agents maneuvering in 2D space.
- **Magnetic Poles (`Magnet`):** Point-source attractive/repulsive forces.
- **Fluid Density (`Platter`):** The underlying grid mechanism (from `ferrous-core`) used for tracking particle density to simulate fluid pressure repulsion.

### From `ripple-tank` (Parent B)
- **Acoustic Wave Grid:** The 2D wave equation simulation.
- **Audio Synthesis:** The `resonance-audio` backend that sonifies the wave grid.
- **Rendering:** The `macroquad` rendering loop.

### The Novel Trait (Emergence)
**Magnetic Acoustic Resonance.** The two systems are coupled in a bidirectional feedback loop:
1. **Acoustic Advection:** The wave pressure gradients act as physical forces pushing the magnetic particles. Sound physically moves matter.
2. **Kinetic Plucking:** When particles reach high velocities (e.g., when snapping toward a strong magnet), their kinetic energy deposits "plucks" into the acoustic wave tank. Matter creates sound.

The result is a dynamic, resonant system where magnetic fields organize particles, and the resulting particle movement generates acoustic waves that further disrupt and reorganize the particles.
