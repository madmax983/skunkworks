# Ferrous Tissue 🧲🥩

A hybrid experiment combining **Soft Body Physics** (Chimera Tissue) with **Magnetic Cellular Automata** (Ferrous Genesis).

## 🧬 Lineage

- **Parent A**: `experiments/chimera-tissue` (Programmable Matter, Soft Body Physics)
- **Parent B**: `experiments/ferrous-genesis` (Magnetic Particles, Cellular Automata)

## 🔬 Concept

This experiment simulates a "Piezo-Magnetic Organism".
- The body is a mesh of particles connected by "Muscle Fibers" (Actuators).
- Each particle is also a magnetic dipole (North/South).
- The organism's DNA controls both the contraction of muscles and the magnetic polarity of its cells.

## ✨ Novel Traits

- **Emergent Piezoelectricity**: Mechanical strain feeds into the genetic program, which can trigger a change in magnetic polarity.
- **Magnetostriction**: Magnetic fields (sensed by the organism) can trigger muscle contraction.
- **Self-Assembly**: The organism can use magnetic forces to fold or stiffen itself, in addition to mechanical muscle contraction.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Up/Down Arrow**: Zoom In/Out
- The organism evolves and moves autonomously.

## 🧠 Genetic Logic

The default DNA implements a feedback loop:
1. **Strain -> Magnetism**: If the tissue is stretched, it becomes magnetic.
2. **Magnetism -> Contraction**: If the tissue is magnetic, it contracts.

This creates a complex interplay where mechanical stress travels through the body as magnetic waves.
