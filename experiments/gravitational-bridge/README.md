# Gravitational Bridge

**Lineage:** `biomimetic-bridge` × `harmony-of-spheres`

## Concept
A simulation where ants (agents) live on orbiting planets (N-body physics). When planets pass close to each other, the ants form living bridges (spring constraints) to connect them.

## Parents
- **experiments/biomimetic-bridge**: Provided the concept of ants building structures based on local conditions.
- **experiments/harmony-of-spheres**: Provided the N-body physics engine and procedural audio.

## Emerging Behavior
Bridges form during close encounters but are stretched to the breaking point as planets orbit away. The soundscape reflects the formation and snapping of these cosmic structures.

## Controls
- **Left Click**: Spawn a new planet (and ants) at the mouse position.
