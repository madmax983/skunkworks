# Gravitational Typography ⚛️📜

A hybrid experiment combining Fluid Dynamics and General Relativity (simulated).
Text particles flow in a fluid medium that is warped by the gravity of massive bodies.

## Lineage
- **Parent A**: `experiments/typographic-turbulence` (Fluid Simulation + Text Particles)
- **Parent B**: `experiments/lensing-poetry` (N-Body Physics + Gravitational Lensing Shader)

## Concept
- **Fluid Gravity**: The fluid velocity field is influenced by the gravitational pull of massive bodies.
- **Lensing**: The background texture is distorted by a GLSL shader mimicking gravitational lensing.
- **Text Advection**: Characters typed by the user float in the fluid, spiraling into gravity wells.

## Controls
- **Typing**: Spawns text particles at the mouse cursor.
- **Left Click**: Spawns a massive body (Star/Black Hole).
- **F**: Toggle Fluid Visualization (Density/Flow).
- **UP/DOWN**: Increase/Decrease Lensing Strength.

## Emergent Behavior
- Accretion disks form around massive bodies as text particles spiral in.
- The "Event Horizon" effect where lensing distorts the particles beyond recognition.
