# 🧬 Gray DDoS

**Experiment Type:** Swarm Intelligence / Reaction-Diffusion / Cybersecurity Visualization
**Status:** [FRESH]
**Stack:** Rust, Macroquad, Rayon

A visual simulation of a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack utilizing a Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion substrate.

## 🧬 Lineage

- **Parent A:** `experiments/locust-ddos` (Swarm intelligence seeking targets, firewalls)
- **Parent B:** `crates/gray-scott` (Reaction-Diffusion chemical simulation)
- **Novel Trait:** Morphogenetic Cyberwarfare. Instead of simple pheromones, the DDoS packets deposit chemical "V" (the predator chemical). The firewalls emit chemical "U" (the prey/neutralizer chemical) and alter the diffusion rate. The DDoS botnet navigates the continuous Turing patterns to find optimal routes to the target, appearing as an organic, spreading fungus being burned back by chemical firewalls.

## Controls

- **Left Click:** Deploy a Chemical Firewall (High kill rate zone).
- **C:** Clear all Firewalls.
