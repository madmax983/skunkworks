# Forensic Analysis: `hamming-garden`

## Terminal Characteristics

**Documentation Void:** The specimen lacks a `README.md`. No instructions, manifesto, or hypothesis are provided.

**Static Stagnation:** The implementation relies on a static "SEED_TEXT" and performs a basic grid-based visualization of bit flips and corrections. While the concept of visualizing Hamming distance as "entropy vs gardener" has merit, the execution is minimal and lacks the emergent complexity found in superior specimens like `cellular-beats` or `fissure-tracker`.

**Redundancy:** The "decay" and "entropy" niche is already occupied by `fissure-tracker` (geological decay), `digital-compost` (file decay), and `reaction-monitor` (chemical decay). This specimen adds no unique mechanical or visual voice to the ecosystem.

**Execution Quality:** The TUI is a simple character grid. Interaction is limited to adjusting rate constants. It resembles a "Hello World" for cellular automata rather than a fully realized experiment.

## Verdict

**CONDEMN.**
