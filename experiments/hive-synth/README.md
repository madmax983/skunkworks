# Hive Synth 🐝🎹

**Genetic Cross**: `thermo-tarmites` × `cellular-beats`

A musical cellular automaton where "termites" rearrange notes to optimize thermal efficiency, creating emergent musical structures.

## Lineage
-   **Allele A (thermo-tarmites)**: Agents (termites) move materials based on local heat gradients (simulated thermodynamics).
-   **Allele B (cellular-beats)**: A grid scanning audio sequencer that plays notes based on cell state.
-   **Emergent Trait**: Termites build cooling structures around "Servers" (Heat sources). These structures are interpreted as musical notes by the sequencer, creating a self-organizing melody.

## Controls
-   **Space**: Play/Pause Sequencer
-   **Enter**: Toggle Simulation
-   **+/-**: Adjust Tempo
-   **q**: Quit

## Concept
Servers generate "Heat" (Server Load). Termites pick up "Notes" (Cooling Blocks) and place them to dissipate heat.
The sequencer scans the world from left to right.
-   **Y-Axis**: Pitch (Chromatic Scale)
-   **X-Axis**: Time
