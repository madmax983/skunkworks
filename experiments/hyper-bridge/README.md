# Hyper Bridge 🌉🌌

A hybrid experiment combining **Biomimetic Bridge** and **Tesseract Ops**.

## 🧬 Lineage

*   **Parent A:** `experiments/biomimetic-bridge` (Ant Colony Optimization, Bridge Building)
*   **Parent B:** `experiments/tesseract-ops` (4D Hypercube, System Monitoring, Metric Visualization)
*   **Genetic Engineer:** The Splice Surgeon

## 🔬 Concept

Ants inhabit the edges of a 4D Hypercube (Tesseract). The geometry of the Tesseract is driven by system metrics:
*   **CPU:** Scales the X dimension.
*   **RAM:** Scales the Y dimension.
*   **Swap:** Scales the Z dimension.
*   **Load Average:** Scales the W dimension (pulsing) and drives the rotation speed.

As the system comes under load, the Tesseract expands and distorts. The ants, sensing the increased distance and stress, build **Hyper-Bridges** (cyan edges) to connect distant vertices, creating shortcuts and reinforcing the structure.

These bridges decay over time, but persist longer if the system remains under load.

## 🕹️ Controls

*   **Arrow Keys:** Rotate Camera (3D projection)
*   **W/S:** Zoom In/Out
*   **System Load:** Physically expands the universe.

## 🧪 Phenotype

*   **Structure:** 4D Hypercube projected to 3D.
*   **Agents:** Ants traversing edges.
*   **Emergence:** When the computer works hard, the hypercube blooms with cyan bridges as the colony tries to maintain connectivity across the expanding void.
