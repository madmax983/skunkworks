## The Law

**A hold is one grain deep until the next grain strikes it.**
The lagging cave's ceiling stands in
(0.5314453125, 0.53154296875]: the cave that crossed in the
first half of four consecutive intervals and then held once
was not standing on a floor — it was standing on the one
train the five-hundred-and-twelfth could not price, and the
next halving took that train too. And the pooled pair count
cannot price a crossing: at this strike it fell while the
cave crossed, because weather in the opposed cave outweighs
one train. Read the solo ledger for the verdict (B-only
1 → 0) and the pair count for the weather — never the other
way round.

**Falsification condition:** the one-thousand-and-twenty-fourth
claim — that shallow + wide's ceiling stands in
(0.53154296875, 0.531640625], with the twin cave's and wide +
shallow's B-only staying 0 at the one-thousand-and-twenty-fourth
— dies if, at σ 0.10 at ONETHOUSANDTWENTYFOURTH, shallow + wide's
B-only reads 0 (its ceiling in the first half of its
five-hundred-and-twelfth after all), or either crossed cave's
B-only reopens above 0, or if the twinjoint judge errs or
misnames in a clean cave at either level, or if its clean-cave
ρ error at ONETHOUSANDTWENTYFOURTH is anything but the grid's
0.01845703125, or if the twin cave stops being the dearest cave
at a level.
