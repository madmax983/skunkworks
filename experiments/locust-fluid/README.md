# Locust Fluid 🌊🦟

**A Hybrid Experiment by The Splice Surgeon**

> "A liquid swarm. Packets behave as a compressible fluid seeking a target."

## 🔬 Experiment Analysis

This experiment combines swarm intelligence (DDoS packets seeking a target) with a continuous fluid simulation (magnetic/ferrous fluid).

- **Parent A**: `experiments/locust-ddos` (Swarm Intelligence / Seeking Behavior)
- **Parent B**: `experiments/ferrous-fluid` (Continuous Fluid Simulation / Physics)

### Concept
Instead of simple swarm agents, the packets are simulated as fluid particles. When attacking a server, the packets compress into a high-density fluid, actively pushing against each other and visualizing the pressure wave of a DDoS attack against firewalls.

### Lineage & Genetics
- **Allele A (Behavior)**: Target seeking, firewall avoidance, congestion pheromones.
- **Allele B (Physics)**: Fluid particle simulation, pressure, viscosity, continuous space mapping.
- **Emergent Trait**: Fluid-Dynamic Bottlenecking. The packets visually bunch up into a compressed fluid wave when they encounter firewalls, displaying emergent fluid turbulence rather than discrete bouncing particles.
