- **Parent A (Structure):** `crates/market-sim`
  - *Inherited Traits:* The continuous physical double auction market logic. Bids (buyers) and Asks (sellers) moving spatially to execute trades.
- **Parent B (Medium):** `experiments/ferrous-fluid`
  - *Inherited Traits:* The magnetic particle physics engine, continuous 2D fluid simulation (Platter), viscous drag, and particle locomotion.
- **Emergent Phenotype:** **Magnetic Liquidity**.
  - Instead of standard market participants, Bids and Asks act as active magnetic poles in a ferrous fluid.
  - Bids attract other Bids but repel Asks, while Asks attract Asks but repel Bids.
  - As trades execute and remove opposing poles, the surrounding fluid structurally collapses into the void, causing self-organizing magnetic liquidity pools to snap together dynamically.

The market's discrete intent (buying and selling) becomes a continuous, structural force field shaping a physical fluid.
