# Market Resonance

## Acoustic Market Sonification

`market-resonance` bridges discrete market events with continuous acoustic waves. It hybridizes `market-sim` and `resonance-audio`, converting market limit orders (bids and asks) that collide into simulated acoustic waves. Each collision "plucks" the continuous audio membrane, mapping market volatility into generative acoustic soundscapes.

## Headless Mode
Pass `--headless` to safely run the program in CI environments without attempting to spawn a graphical display context.
