# Acoustic Market Dynamics (market-tank)

A hybrid experiment combining a continuous double-auction particle system with physical acoustic wave mechanics.

## Lineage
- **Parent A**: `crates/market-sim` (Continuous Double Auction Particle System)
- **Parent B**: `experiments/ripple-tank` (Physical Acoustic Wave Mechanics)
- **Concept**: Acoustic Market Dynamics. The discrete market events (trades) inject physical acoustic energy into a 2D wave tank.
- **Novel Trait**: Acoustic Liquidity. Trade executions pluck the acoustic tank, sending pressure waves that literally push resting Bids and Asks around, simulating market volatility driven by physical sound waves.
