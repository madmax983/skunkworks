## The Novel Trait

Every address priced on the western leg before this planting was
an end or a tread address: 0, +150, +225, +375. The two intervals
between them — (0, 150] and (225, 375] — had never had their
midpoints read. This planting keeps the evidence room's packets,
re-judgement, aversion and acquittal rule verbatim, books caution
by source exactly as `westedge-moths` does, expires **both**
pools at lifetime **750**, and sets the one offset to **+75** or
**+300** — in three rooms at each address:

- **Staggered-75 / -300** — taught units expire at gain + 750 +
  offset; earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored-75 / -300** — earned units expire at gain + 750 +
  offset; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, at midleg.
- **Coincident-75 / -300 (the controls)** — *both* pools expire
  at gain + 750 + offset. The deadlines stay coincident with
  each other; only the duration is longer (longest deadlines
  825 / 1050). At zero offset the coincident control *is*
  waning-750 (16); at +375 the coincident-both room reads 13.

One hundred and seven twin rooms, same seed: the one hundred
and one parents, unchanged (tests pin their ledgers exactly —
including staggered-471 fed 50 / burns 10, the split itself, and
the western tread and valley entire), plus the six midleg rooms.
The moths know only what the field delivers and what it cost
them; offsets are the keeper's bookkeeping, never the moth's.

**The two lines trade prices at the midpoints.** At +75 the
taught line reads the coincident line's shelf price (13) and
the coincident line reads it too — in the same room, figure
for figure. At +300 the taught line reads the coincident
line's valley floor (10) while the coincident line itself
has already climbed out to 12. Read whole, the leg is not a
stair with a valley beside it: it is two lines crossing and
recrossing through prices each was supposed to own, and the
addresses where they agree (0, +75) are exactly the addresses
where a survey of ends and midpoints would swear they were
one line.

**The law:** a midpoint is not an average. Price the middle
of an interval and you may find a price neither end has ever
read — a dip under a tread, a plunge to another line's floor,
a mirror still asleep. The ends of a leg tell you where the
prices start and stop; they tell you nothing about the
country between, and the country between is most of the leg.

## Falsification condition

This planting is wrong if, on another seed or cave, the
midleg rooms separate from these readings (staggered ≠ 13 or
mirrored ≠ 16 or coincident ≠ 13 at +75; staggered ≠ 10 or
mirrored ≠ 11 or coincident ≠ 12 at +300) — that would make
the dip, the sleeping mirror and the narrow valley properties
of this seed's journeys, not of the addresses — or if a room
at +150 or +225 on another seed reads anything but 14 / 11 /
10, which would move the tread and the valley floor
themselves and reopen the west entire.
