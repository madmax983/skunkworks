# miller-physics

**Parents**: `crates/miller-lattice` × `crates/physics-pbd`

## Concept
Codebase Physical Lattice Morphogenesis. The discrete 3D crystalline lattice generated by filesystem structures (`miller-lattice`) is subjected to continuous soft-body constraints (`physics-pbd`). Directories are modeled as heavier particles, files as lighter particles, and their hierarchical parent-child relationships are mapped to physical distance constraints.

## Novel Trait
Structural Codebase Morphogenesis. The 3D hierarchical lattice of the codebase acts as a soft-body organism. The root directory is pinned, and the rest of the structure dangles or floats around it. As the physics simulation steps, the distance constraints between directories and files attempt to resolve against their initial positions, creating a bouncing, breathing physical representation of the repository's architecture.

cargo run -p miller-physics
