## The Novel Trait

Every staggered room so far offset the **taught** pool — the
lessons a moth was given by other moths' checks. This planting
keeps the evidence room's packets, re-judgement, aversion and
acquittal rule verbatim, books caution by source exactly as
`staggered-moths` does, expires **both** pools at lifetime
**750**, and moves the one offset to the other pool:

- **Mirrored** — earned units expire at gain + 750 + 375;
  taught units at gain + 750. Co-gained units expire 375 ticks
  apart, exactly as in the staggered room; only *which*
  forgetting waits has changed. The earned pool is the pool of
  scars — a moth's own burns and the burns it witnessed — so
  the mirror asks whether a scar that waits protects like a
  lesson that waits.

Twenty twin rooms, same seed: the nineteen parents, unchanged
(tests pin their ledgers exactly — including staggered fed 50 /
burns 9, expired 55 taught / 15 earned; staggered-both fed 45 /
burns 13; waning-750 fed 48 / burns 16), plus the mirrored room.
`staggered-both` already serves as the coincident control at
the longest deadline (1125) for both staggers, so no second
control is planted. The moths know only what the field delivers
and what it cost them; offsets are the keeper's bookkeeping,
never the moth's.

The censuses, kept beside the burns as this lineage has learned

and fewer earned (12 against 15), yet burns only 2 more. The
ordering across the four two-pool rooms is now complete and
strict: **staggered 9 < mirrored 11 < coincident-late 13 <
coincident-750 16**. Symmetry holds in kind — both staggers sit
below both coincident rooms — and fails in exact price by
exactly the 2 burns that separate the two ways of waiting. A
scar that waits protects nearly like a lesson that waits;
nearly is the measured word.

**The law:** a stagger is symmetric in kind and asymmetric in
price. Before blaming a lifetime, stagger it — and before
calling the stagger pool-blind, mirror it: which forgetting
waits is worth 2 burns in this cave, and the waiting apart is
worth 4 against coincidence in both directions. Two forgettings
that land together still cost more than either costs alone,
whichever of them you hold back.

## Falsification condition

This planting is wrong if, on another seed or cave, the
mirrored room burns at or above the coincident control at the
same longest deadline (mirrored ≥ staggered-both) — that would
make the earned stagger no stagger at all — or if the mirror
gap (mirrored − staggered) grows past the coincidence gap
itself (4 burns) on a second seed, which would demote the
waiting apart to a minor term beside which pool waits.
