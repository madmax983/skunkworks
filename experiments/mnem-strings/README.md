# Mnemonic Strings 🎻🍂

**Lineage:** `mnem-rot` (Codebase Decay) × `ferrous-strings` (Acoustic Magnetic Flow).

## Concept

This experiment hybridizes the active, structural visual decay of a codebase graph (`mnem-rot`) with physical magnetic acoustic strings (`ferrous-strings`).
The graph nodes in the codebase act as kinetic agents. Instead of being perfectly stable, their entropy introduces chaotic velocity and erratic paths.

As rotting, high-entropy codebase nodes fly past the set of strings, they actively pluck them. Their level of decay (entropy) dictates the intensity of the strike, translating the codebase's rot directly into physical sound and pushing magnetic anomalies onto the background canvas. A well-maintained codebase remains quiet, while an ignored repository creates an acoustic cacophony mapping its failing health to high-frequency string oscillations and magnetic field interference patterns.

## Controls

*   **Right Click + Drag:** Pan the camera.
*   **Scroll:** Zoom in/out.
*   **Hover:** Heal a node and view its (potentially corrupted) content. This lowers its entropy, making it stop striking strings and calm the space.

## Technical Details

*   **Graph Decay:** Adapted from `mnem-rot`.
*   **Physics Strings:** The `Platter` and acoustic systems taken from `ferrous-strings` serve as fixed instruments embedded in the digital space.

## The Novel Trait
"Acoustic Manifestation of Code Decay." The physical acoustic sound translates the codebase's metadata intent and overall health into a dynamic audio-visual representation via string simulation.
