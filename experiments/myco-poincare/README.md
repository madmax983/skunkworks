# myco-poincare
### Concept
Hyperbolic Foraging.

### Novel trait
Slime mold agents navigate the continuous space of a Poincaré disk. As they approach the boundary, hyperbolic distance stretches the physical distances, creating an environment where agents appear to slow down and condense near the edge while moving freely in the center.

### Predicted Phenotype
An organic, mycelial network mapped over hyperbolic space, with incredibly dense, chaotic pathways formed at the edge of the disk while the center holds broad, sweeping biological highways.
