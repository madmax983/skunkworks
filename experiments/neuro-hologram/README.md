# neuro-hologram

**Parents:** `crates/neuro-sim` × `experiments/hologram-text`

## Concept
Holographic Neural Networks. A Spiking Neural Network (SNN) where the continuous membrane voltages of the Izhikevich neurons are treated as a spatial density field and transformed via 2D FFT into an optical interference pattern.

## Novel Trait
Spectral Neural Activity. We view the discrete neural spiking entirely through its frequency domain interference pattern via FFT.

## Predicted Phenotype
An emergent spectral neural visualization. Synchronized bursts of spiking activity (like spreading depression or seizures) will create standing waves and high-frequency turbulence in the optical domain.
