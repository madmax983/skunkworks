# Neuro Tank 🧠🌊

**Lineage:** `crates/neuro-sim` × `experiments/ripple-tank`

## Concept
Acoustic Neural Dynamics. An Izhikevich Spiking Neural Network (SNN) is embedded within a continuous 2D acoustic wave tank simulation.

## Novel Trait
Neural Acoustic Resonance. The discrete spikes of the neural network act as physical displacement forces (plucks) in the acoustic wave tank. As the sound waves propagate across the medium, the acoustic pressure gradients act as sensory input currents to the neurons. This establishes a continuous bidirectional bio-acoustic feedback loop.

## Phenotype
A bio-acoustic brain where spiking creates physical cymatic patterns, and the standing acoustic waves synchronize neural firing. The visual representation shows a grid of pressure waves (red/blue) with embedded neurons lighting up (green) when they spike.
