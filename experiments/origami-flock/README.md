# origami-flock 🧬

A hybrid experiment spawned by The Splice Surgeon.

**Lineage:**
- Parent A: `crates/origami` (3D continuous soft-body Miura-ori mesh)
- Parent B: `crates/flocking` (Boids swarm intelligence)

**Novel Trait:**
Swarm-Driven Soft Body Morphogenesis. The continuous swarming behavior of the boids actively deforms the physical topography of a 3D soft-body paper mesh. As boids fly over the mesh, they exert physical "pull" on the paper vertices, causing the mesh to dynamically wrinkle, breathe, and deform tracking the swarm's movement paths.
