# Origami Lexicon 🧬🦢🗣️

A hybrid experiment combining **Manifold Origami** (Physics-based folding) and **Biomorphic Lexicon** (Linguistic evolution).

## Concept

Words are inscribed on the faces of a Miura-ori fold pattern. As you fold the paper, distant parts of the text are brought into physical contact. When "Semantic Cells" (faces containing text) collide, they exchange phonemes and mutate according to linguistic rules (Grimm's Law, Vowel Shift), simulating a "Combinatorial Philology" where meaning is created by the topology of the medium.

## Controls

- **UP / DOWN Arrows**: Fold / Unfold the paper.
- **Mouse Drag**: Rotate Camera.
- **Scroll**: Zoom.
- **R**: Reset simulation.

## Novel Trait

**Topological Linguistics**: The evolution of the text is driven by the physical folding of the medium. Meaning is not just sequential but spatial and topological.
