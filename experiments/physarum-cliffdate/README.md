# physarum-cliffdate 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-migration × the cliff inside
its fifth slice (one ground, one break, six dates)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-migration → the unmarked
hour inside ticks 8,400..9,000 where the plateau ended. The
parent dated the tenure at 300-tick grain and found a cliff at
its boundary; the bearing runs from the slice that concealed
the break to the hundred ticks that hold it. The farthest
corner of this map is, for the sixth time, a *when*, not a
*where* — but this time the when is a hundred ticks wide.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-migration` — the Cartographer's twenty-third survey,
which carried `physarum-pastseason`'s machinery whole and added
a slice ledger across the past window — closed with a tenure
dated, but coarsely. City 8's collection ran on a plateau —
1,469 / 1,654 / 1,650 / **1,966** ghost visits across slices
1–4 — and then broke: **212** in slice 5 (8,700..9,000), 17 in
slice 6, 0 in slice 7. The parent's law, its twenty-third
watch: *a ghost's tenure is a plateau with a cliff, not a slope
and not a spike.* Its closing seed asks where inside the
plateau's last slice and the cliff's first slice the break
actually stands, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim:
*the break at finer grain — city 8's collection fell 1,966 →
212 between slices 4 and 5: at 100-tick grain inside
8,400..9,000, does the cliff have a single tick, and does city
1's slice-4 burst (333) sit before or after the plateau's last
full slice?*

## The novel trait

Migration's machinery is carried whole — agents as points in
the unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on the
projection with the void drinking at the rim, the mold's
boundary rule, the opposed channels stone for stone (loud
stamp 1.0 eaten at 0.004, quiet stamp 0.40 eaten at 0.010), the
same seed, the same nine cities at the same disk addresses,
the same fifteen rooms at the same five lengths, and
migration's 300-tick slice ledger entire — and exactly one
instrument is added:

- **The fine ledger.** In every room long enough to reach it,
  cumulative per-city ghost visits are snapshotted at ticks
  8,400, 8,500, 8,600, 8,700, 8,800, 8,900 and 9,000. The six
  differences are the fine slices spanning the parent's
  slices 4 and 5, printed per city in CurvedPastDepleted's
  ledger. The instrument is exact by construction: the fine
  slices sum, per city, to the parent's slice-4 plus slice-5
  counts, read here by a second path. Nothing about the world
  changes; only the dating improves — a second time, at a
  third of the grain.

The 1,200-, 2,400-, 4,800-, 7,200- and 9,600-tick rooms stand
as controls and must reproduce their parents' pinned ledgers
exact, including migration's slices.

**P2 HELD — the plateau holds to its edge, and declines
gently doing it.** City 8's three fine slices inside the

above the declared 400 floor. The plateau is not flat at

**P3 HELD — the cliff is a step.** City 8 falls **613 → 121**
across the single step at tick 8,700 — an 80% fall inside one
hundred-tick cell, the 8700..8800 count at 19.7% of the
preceding cell, under the declared 25% cap — and the tail
does not rebound: **87**, then **4**. What the 300-tick grain
called a cliff (1,966 → 212) is, at hundred-tick grain, one
step and a two-cell tail; the working life of the tenure ends
at 8,700 entire, not across the slice.

**P4 HELD — the burst is a burst.** City 1's largest fine
slice (8,500..8,600) holds **216** of its 333 — **64.9%**,
above the declared 60% — with the remaining 117 in the next
cell and nothing anywhere else in the 600 ticks.

**P5 HELD — the burst precedes the cliff, and sits mid-plateau.**
City 1's whole burst (216 + 117) lies inside 8,400..8,700,
and its peak cell is the plateau's *middle* hundred, not its
last: the first-fallen tried the apron at the plateau's
height, two hundred ticks before the break, not at its edge
and not after it.

**P6 CRACKED — the far side is not the floor's at first.**
In the cliff's own cell (8,700..8,800) city 5 collects
**0** against city 8's 121: the fallen plateau, even falling,
out-collects the constant tenant for two more cells (121 and
87 against 0 and 8). City 5 takes the far side only in the
window's last hundred ticks — **185** in 8,900..9,000,
against city 8's 4 — two hundred ticks after the break. The
succession is slower than the fall: the break is a step, the
arrival is a walk.

**The cliff, dated:** at hundred-tick grain the tenure's end
is three distinct shapes in sequence — a plateau easing
(688 / 665 / 613), a single step (→ 121), and a tail (87 →
4) — while its successor rehearses mid-plateau (216 / 117,
done by 8,700) and its floor arrives late (185, only in the
last hundred). No cell in the 600 is empty, and no fourth
city appears: the break, the rehearsal and the arrival are
the whole census of the window at this grain.

**The law, twenty-fourth watch:** *a cliff, dated finely, is
a step with a tail, not a slope and not an edge: the plateau
eases inside its last slice without ceasing to be a plateau,
falls four-fifths in a single hundred ticks, and tails to
nothing across two more — and the map's next tenant does not
stand at the cliff's foot waiting. The break takes one cell;
the succession takes three. Date a fall and you date only
the fall: the arrival keeps its own, slower calendar.*
