**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-clifftenth × the sag inside
its plateau's last hundred (one ground, one sag, fifty ticks)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-clifftenth → the

one tick wide.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-clifftenth` — the Cartographer's twenty-fifth
survey, which carried `physarum-cliffdate`'s machinery whole
and added a fine ledger at 10-tick grain inside 8,600..8,800 —
closed with the cliff's single hundred-tick step resolved
into four shapes in sequence: a plateau that sags mid-hundred
(city 8: **34 / 38** at 8,670..8,690) and recovers (**49** at
8,690..8,700), a boundary that rises (**56** at 8,700..8,710),
a true break one cell late (across tick 8,710), and a
scattered tail. Its closing seed asks what the sag is made
of, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim: *the sag
at single-tick grain — city 8's plateau sags to 34 / 38 at
8,670..8,690 and recovers to 49 by 8,700: at 1-tick grain
inside 8,660..8,710, is the sag a smooth valley or a gap of
whole silent ticks, and does the recovery cross 50 before
the boundary?*

## The novel trait

Clifftenth's machinery is carried whole — agents as points
in the unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on
the projection with the void drinking at the rim, the mold's
boundary rule, the opposed channels stone for stone (loud
stamp 1.0 eaten at 0.004, quiet stamp 0.40 eaten at 0.010),

addresses, the same fifteen rooms at the same five lengths,
migration's 300-tick slice ledger entire, cliffdate's
100-tick fine ledger entire, and clifftenth's 10-tick tenth
ledger entire — and exactly one instrument is added:

- **The tick ledger.** In every room long enough to reach
  it, cumulative per-city ghost visits are snapshotted every
  tick from 8,660 to 8,710. The fifty differences are the
  tick slices spanning the parent's tenth cells 6..10,
  printed per city in CurvedPastDepleted's ledger. The
  instrument is exact by construction: the tick slices sum,
  per city, to the tenth cells they span, read here by a
  second path. Nothing about the world changes; only the
  dating improves — a fourth time, at a fiftieth of the
  parent's grain.

The 1,200-, 2,400-, 4,800-, 7,200- and 9,600-tick rooms stand
as controls and must reproduce their parents' pinned ledgers
exact, including migration's slices, cliffdate's fine slices,
and clifftenth's tenth slices.

**The sag, taken apart:** at single-tick grain the
plateau's two-cell sag resolves into a descent (10 → 1

ticks is silent, and nothing in them is anyone but city 8.

**The law, twenty-sixth survey:** *a sag at one grain is a
valley at the next, and a valley has a floor, not a gap:
before calling a dip an absence, count its single ticks —
a flow can fall to one visit a tick and still never stop,
and the difference between "nearly gone" and "gone" is
exactly the visits the coarse grain averaged away. And a
recovery can be dated to the tick: this one needed every
one of its forty-nine, and its fiftieth stands on the
boundary stone itself.*
