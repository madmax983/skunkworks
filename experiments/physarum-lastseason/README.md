# physarum-lastseason 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-farseason × the season itself, a
third time (one ground, four lengths of time)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-farseason → the unmarked
country past tick 4,800: the far season's map ends with one lit
address and a projection — a fall near tick 6,600 — and a projection
is not a survey. The bearing runs from the disk's last lit corner
to the hour the map says it should go dark. The farthest corner of
this map is, for the third time, a *when*, not a *where*.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-farseason` — the Cartographer's twentieth survey, which
carried `physarum-longseason`'s machinery whole and doubled its
season again — closed with a law and a survivor. The law, its
twentieth watch: **immortality is priced in arrivals, and an
address can be so far from every road that even an emptying map
cannot route enough journeys past it to spend it in three
doublings of the season.** The far season spent eight of the
disk's nine cities, collecting its last presented bill (loud
city 3, 3.31 of stock) at tick 3,252, in stock order, on 844
far-window visits, and the haunting diluted to 41.6% loud.

The survivor is one address. Disk city 8 — quiet, at the rim —
ended the far season lit with **7.29** of its 25.0 stock unspent,
spent by more than half and spent *faster* than before: **988
visits in the far window** (ticks 2,400..4,800) against 783 in the
whole long season, a 26% rise as the map emptied around it, priced
exact at the quick mouth's 0.010 — 17.17 − 9.88 = 7.29, to the
cent. It has never been haunted, because it has never died. The
parent's closing seed asks whether the projection is a survey or
a hope, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim: *a season
of 7,200 ticks on the disk — at the far window's rate (988 visits
per 2,400 ticks, and rising) city 8's remaining 7.29 projects to
a fall near tick 6,600: does it fall, does the rate keep rising
as the map empties to one address, and if it falls, is its ghost
the last sound on the disk?*

## The novel trait

Farseason's machinery is carried whole — agents as points in the
unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on the
projection with the void drinking at the rim, the mold's boundary
rule, the opposed channels stone for stone (loud stamp 1.0 eaten
at 0.004, quiet stamp 0.40 eaten at 0.010), the same seed, the
same nine cities at the same disk addresses — and the season is
extended once more, to 7,200 ticks, in three new rooms:

- **CurvedLastEternal** (disk, 7,200 ticks, stocks in shadow only)
  and **CurvedLastDepleted** (disk, 7,200 ticks, stock gating):
  does the last lit address end, and what does its ending sound
  like?
- **OpposedLastDepleted** (flat, 7,200 ticks) stands beside them
  as the control ground: flat has had no survivors since tick
  2,400, so its last room prices only how the haunting grows when
  there has been nothing left to spend for two whole seasons.

The 1,200-, 2,400- and 4,800-tick rooms stand as controls and
must reproduce their parents' pinned ledgers exact. A last room's
first 4,800 ticks are its far twin's whole season, sample for
sample — the truncation identity is printed in the ledger and
asserted in the tests, so the last rooms differ from the far ones
only in being allowed to continue. The ledger also prices the
last window directly: each city's visits in ticks 4,800..7,200,
taken as the last room's count minus the far room's — exact, by
that identity.

One instrument addition, declared before the run: the parent
tracked ghost visits in a season's final 300 ticks only as a
total. This cross also prices them **city by city**, so the
last-sound question — whose ghost is still speaking at the end —
has a ledger to answer in, not an impression.

**Falsification condition:** if CurvedLastDepleted ends with city
8 still lit — stock still on its books after 7,200 ticks, three
extensions of the season past the first survey — then the
projection was a hope, not a survey: arrival-priced immortality
at this address is not a season-length artefact at any scale this
lineage can reach, and the visits *floor* seed (a rate below
which no length spends a city) stops being a question and
becomes the map's last law. If instead the disk ends fully dark,
the artefact was the whole story at every length, and the only
question left is the one P5 asks: what the ending sounded like.
Either result stands in the ledger below, in those terms.

## The ledger (headless, as found)

**B1 HELD, exact.** Flat OpposedDepleted reproduces the parent's
pinned ledger field for field: loud visits **73,231**, loud mean
first-dark **268.2**, ghost visits **53,800** (loud 41,917, a 77.9%
share), 8 of 9 dark, final mass **2,237.22** / coverage **3,410**,
city 8 lit with **4.28** unspent.

**B2 HELD, exact, at all three earlier lengths.** The curved
1,200 rooms reproduce `physarum-curved`'s pinned ledger field for
field: eternal loud **36,226** / quiet **4,712** (ratio 6.15),
mass **1,124.80** / coverage **1,908**; depleted **4 of 9** dark,
mass **921.05** / coverage **1,557**. The 2,400 rooms reproduce
`physarum-longseason`'s: CurvedLongDepleted **7 of 9** dark, mass
**940.62** / coverage **1,540**, stock left 20.48, city 8 lit at
**17.17** on **783** visits, city 3 lit at **3.31**;
OpposedLongDepleted **9 of 9** dark. The 4,800 rooms reproduce
`physarum-farseason`'s: CurvedFarDepleted **8 of 9** dark, mass
**452.64** / coverage **733**, stock left **7.29** — every unit of
it city 8's — ghost visits 24,703 at 41.6% loud, city 3 dark at
**3,252**; OpposedFarDepleted **9 of 9** dark. All six truncation
identities print **true**, at all three steps of the ladder.

**P1 HELD — city 8's immortality lapses, and early.** City 8
darkens in CurvedLastDepleted at tick **5,204** — only 404 ticks
into the last window, against the projection's near-6,600. The
projection priced the bill at the far window's *average* rate;
what it did not price is that at tick 4,800 city 8 became the
only lit address on the disk, and a sole stamp collects the whole
map's appetite: the 729 visits its remaining 7.29 required at the
quick mouth's 0.010 arrived in 404 ticks, a rate above 1.8 visits
per tick against the far window's 0.41. The exemption was real
for four lengths — 1,200 / 2,400 / 4,800 / and 404 ticks of the
fourth extension — and it ended not when the season grew long
enough but when the map grew empty enough.

**P2 HELD — the arrivals concentrated past the prediction.**
City 8's last-window visits are **1,326** against the far
window's **988** and the long season's **783** — the rate rose at
every length, and the final rise was the steepest: priced by
window, 783, then 988, then 1,326, with the fall itself inside
the last window's first sixth. Concentration is not a metaphor
in this ledger; it is a count, and the count accelerates as the
map empties.

**P3 HELD — the disk ends fully dark.** CurvedLastDepleted ends
**9 of 9** dark: dark ticks **48,835**, stock left **0.00** of
225, final mass **509.72** / coverage **831** — the field itself
*thicker* than at 4,800 (452.64 / 733), fed by a sole stamp for
404 ticks and by the residue of a full map's haunting after.
No lit address remains on the disk. The farthest corner of this
map has been surveyed, and it is dark like everywhere else.

**P4 HELD — length is the only difference, a third time.** Both
new truncation identities print true: the last rooms' first
4,800 ticks are the far seasons sample for sample, mass series
and first_dark alike, on both grounds. The last window printed
in the ledger is therefore priced exact, by difference.

**P5 CRACKED — the last sound is a duet, not a solo.** In
CurvedLastDepleted's final 300 ticks, ghost visits total **428**
— against the far season's single ghost visit in its last 300 —
and city 8 does hold the largest share: **230**. But loud city 5
holds **195** and quiet city 4 holds **3**: the last city to fall
is the haunting's loudest voice, not its only one. A dead city's
apron keeps drawing journeys on the residue of the field, and
city 5's apron — fed for 7,200 ticks by the map's busiest roads —
never stopped. The prediction asked for a solo; the disk ends
in a duet with an unattended third chair.

**P6 CRACKED — the haunting concentrates instead of diluting.**
The loud share of ghost visits in CurvedLastDepleted is
**43.9%** (loud 11,993 of 27,306), *above* the far season's
41.6% — the dilution that ran for three lengths (100% → 43.0% →
41.6%) reverses at the fourth. While cities remained to fall,
each fall diluted the haunting toward the fallen's class; once
nothing is left alive, the haunting's composition is set by the
dead map's geometry — which aprons the residue still feeds — and
that geometry favours the loud addresses. City 8's own haunting
(596 ghost visits, its first ever) joined the quiet side and was
outweighed anyway.

**P7 HELD — the order survives the last season.** Effective
mean first-dark in CurvedLastDepleted: loud **1,205.4** against
quiet **2,484.5**. Advertisement first, on any ground, at any
length surveyed — four lengths running, and now with no
survivor left to exempt from the comparison.

**P8 HELD — the gate collects even the last address.** In
CurvedLastEternal, where nothing gates, city 8's shadow stock
*never* exhausts by 7,200: **14.67** unspent on **1,033** visits
— against its gated room's **3,097** visits and a fall at 5,204.
The inheritance multiplier at the last address is roughly
threefold: the gated room's city 8 received three visits for
every one the shadow room's did, because in the gated room every
other city's death rerouted its traffic to the survivor. The
collector was never time. The collector was the gate.

**The law, twenty-first watch:** a season of 7,200 ticks spends
the disk's last city at tick 5,204 — not at the projected 6,600,
because the projection priced an average rate and the rate is
not a property of the city but of the map around it: the emptier
the map, the faster the survivor is found, and the last survivor
is found fastest of all. *On curved ground, immortality is
priced in arrivals; and arrivals are priced in company — an
address is immortal exactly as long as it has neighbours whose
stamps divide the journeys, and the farthest corner of the map
was never a place or a rate, but a crowd that finally went home.*
What remains when the crowd is gone: a haunting of 27,306 ghost
visits whose loud share rose again to 43.9%, and a final 300
ticks in which the last sound is a duet — the last city fallen
(230) and the busiest loud apron (195) speaking over each other
into the void.
