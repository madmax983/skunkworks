# physarum-longseason 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-curved × the season itself (a cross of
one ground with two lengths of time)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-curved → the unmarked country past
tick 1,200: every map in this lineage ends at the same surveyed edge, and
the most interesting sentence in the last survey was a question about
what the edge was hiding. The bearing runs from the disk's surveyed
interior to its own future.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-curved` — the revival cross that gave the Poincaré disk the
flat parent's finished instrument (finite stock, two foods, opposed
channels, a seeded stream, a pinned ledger) — closed its survey with a
finding and an artefact-shaped doubt. The finding, its eighteenth watch:
**curvature taxes the mouth, not the stamp.** Priced in relative terms
the stamp came off better on the disk (eternal per-city visit ratio 6.15
against the flat 5.58); priced in arrivals the mouth collapsed (total
eternal visits 40,938 against the flat 152,106 — 27%), the quick mouth
spent *none* of the four quiet cities in 1,200 ticks, and immortality
expanded from the flat ground's one rationed address (city 8, lit with
4.28 unspent) to a whole class plus one loud rim city (city 3, lit with
10.22 unspent). The haunting stayed 100% loud, because only the stamp's
cities died.

The doubt is written into the parent's own closing seeds: every one of
those numbers was measured inside a 1,200-tick season. A mouth that
arrives slowly is not the same as a mouth that never arrives — given
twice the season, the arrivals that did land keep landing, stocks keep
draining at the same per-visit price, and the question is whether the
disk's class-wide immortality is a law of curved ground or merely a
photograph taken before the bill came due.

The parent's closing seed, taken up verbatim: *a longer season on the
disk (2,400 ticks) — does the mouth's reach recover with time, or is
the disk's class-wide immortality a season-length artefact?*

## The novel trait

Curved's machinery is carried whole — agents as points in the unit
disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on the projection with
the void drinking at the rim, the mold's boundary rule, the opposed
channels stone for stone (loud stamp 1.0 eaten at 0.004, quiet stamp
0.40 eaten at 0.010), the same seed, the same nine cities at the same
disk addresses — and the season is doubled in two new rooms:

- **CurvedLongEternal** (disk, 2,400 ticks, stocks in shadow only) and
  **CurvedLongDepleted** (disk, 2,400 ticks, stock gating): who else
  ends when the season doubles, and in what order?
- **OpposedLongDepleted** (flat, 2,400 ticks) stands beside them as
  the control ground: the flat season's own immortal, city 8, held
  4.28 unspent at tick 1,200 — does flat immortality also fall given
  time, or is the flat rationing of a different kind?

The 1,200-tick rooms (flat OpposedDepleted; the curved pair) stand as
controls and must reproduce the parents' pinned ledgers exact. A long
room's first 1,200 ticks are its short twin's whole season, sample for
sample — the truncation identity is printed in the ledger and asserted
in the tests, so the long rooms differ from the short ones only in
being allowed to continue.

## Predictions, declared before the first run

- **B1:** flat OpposedDepleted reproduces `physarum-opposed`'s pinned
  ledger exact — loud visits 73,231, loud mean first-dark 268.2, ghost
  visits 53,800 (loud 41,917), 8 of 9 dark, final mass 2,237.22 /
  coverage 3,410, city 8 lit with 4.28 unspent.
- **B2:** the curved 1,200 rooms reproduce `physarum-curved`'s pinned
  ledger exact — eternal loud 36,226 / quiet 4,712, mass 1,124.80 /
  coverage 1,908; depleted 4 of 9 dark, mass 921.05 / coverage 1,557.
- **P1 (the mouth recovers in part):** CurvedLongDepleted ends with
  strictly more than 4 dark cities — time buys the mouth at least one
  more city.
- **P2 (a quiet city falls):** at least one quiet city darkens on the
  disk by tick 2,400 — the class-wide immortality is at least partly a
  season-length artefact.
- **P3 (immortality survives, rationed again):** at least one quiet
  city is still lit at tick 2,400 — the artefact shrinks the class's
  exemption; it does not repeal it in one doubling.
- **P4 (the thinnest stock falls first):** the first quiet darkening
  in CurvedLongDepleted is city 4, the quiet city holding the least
  stock (5.06 of 25.0) at tick 1,200 — the long season collects its
  bills in the order the short season left them.
- **P5 (flat's immortal falls too):** OpposedLongDepleted ends 9 of 9
  dark — city 8's flat immortality is also a season-length artefact,
  and a shorter-lived one than the disk's.
- **P6 (the haunting dilutes):** the loud share of ghost visits in
  CurvedLongDepleted falls below the short season's 100.0% — once a
  quiet city can die on the disk, the haunting is no longer the
  stamp's exclusive property.
- **P7 (the order survives):** in CurvedLongDepleted the loud class's
  effective mean first-dark tick (never-dark counted as 2,400) stays
  below the quiet class's — doubling the season does not repeal the
  parent's sequence: advertisement first, on any ground, at any
  length.

**Falsification condition:** if CurvedLongDepleted ends with exactly
the short season's four dark cities and the same five lit, then the
disk's immortality is not a season-length artefact at this scale — the
mouth's reach does not recover with time, and what curvature took it
does not give back. If instead every quiet city darkens, the artefact
was the whole story and the disk is merely a slower flatland. Either
result stands in the ledger below, in those terms.

## The ledger (headless, as found)

**B1 HELD, exact.** Flat OpposedDepleted reproduces the parent's
pinned ledger field for field: loud visits **73,231**, loud mean
first-dark **268.2**, ghost visits **53,800** (loud 41,917, a 77.9%
share), 8 of 9 dark, final mass **2,237.22** / coverage **3,410**,
city 8 lit with **4.28** unspent.

**B2 HELD, exact.** The curved 1,200 rooms reproduce
`physarum-curved`'s pinned ledger field for field: eternal loud
**36,226** / quiet **4,712** (ratio 6.15), mass **1,124.80** /
coverage **1,908**; depleted **4 of 9** dark, mass **921.05** /
coverage **1,557**, stock left 64.48, ghost visits 2,835 (100.0%
loud), the four quiet cities lit at 15.83 / 5.06 / 15.92 / 17.45 and
loud city 3 lit at 10.22. Both truncation identities print **true**:
a long room's first 1,200 ticks are its short twin's whole season,
sample for sample.

**P1 HELD — the mouth recovers in part.** CurvedLongDepleted ends
with **7 of 9** cities dark, against the short season's 4: dark ticks
**9,291**, stock left **20.48** of 225, final mass **940.62** /
coverage **1,540**. Doubling the season buys the mouth three more
cities — every one of them in the second half (first new darkening
at tick 1,445).

**P2 HELD — a quiet city falls; three do.** The quiet class's
century of exemption ends in the long room: city 4 darkens at
**1,445**, city 6 at **1,633**, city 2 at **1,656** (quiet mean
first-dark 1,578.0 among the fallen). The class-wide immortality of
the 1,200-tick survey *was* partly a season-length artefact: the
arrivals were landing all along — 17,971 quiet visits in the long
room against the short room's 4,574 — they simply had not finished
paying by the time the short map ended.

**P3 HELD — immortality survives, rationed to one address.** City 8,
the quiet city at the disk's rim address, ends the long season lit
with **17.17** of 25.0 unspent — barely touched, because almost
nobody arrives: **783 visits in 2,400 ticks**, the least-visited city
on the disk by a factor of three. Its exemption is not a thicker
stock or a slower mouth; it is an absence of journeys. (Its loud
mirror in survival, city 3, also ends lit — but spent down to 3.31
from 10.22: visited, billed, and nearly collected.)

**P4 HELD — the thinnest stock falls first.** The first quiet
darkening of the long season is **city 4 at tick 1,445** — the city
holding the least stock (5.06) when the short season closed. The
long season collects its bills in the order the short season left
them: 5.06-stock city 4 first, then 15.92-stock city 6 (1,633), then
15.83-stock city 2 (1,656) — the near-tied pair falls in near-tied
order, visits breaking the tie (city 6's 8,888 visits against city
2's 5,338). In the eternal room's shadow ledger city 4's stock also
exhausts first among the quiet, at tick 1,276.

**P5 HELD — flat's immortal falls, and falls early.** 
OpposedLongDepleted ends **9 of 9** dark: city 8, the flat ground's
rationed immortal, darkens at tick **1,239** — thirty-nine ticks into
the second half of the season. Flat immortality was a season-length
artefact entire, and a shorter-lived one than the disk's: on flat
ground the doubling leaves no survivors at all (final mass 2,216.69 /
coverage 3,379, ghost visits 133,863).

**P6 HELD — the haunting dilutes.** Ghost visits in
CurvedLongDepleted total **16,974**, and the loud share is **43.0%**
(quiet ghosts 9,674) — far below the short season's exclusive 100.0%.
Once a quiet city can die on the disk, it can be haunted there too;
the haunting stops being the stamp's property the moment the mouth's
cities start ending.

**P7 HELD — the order survives the long season.** Effective mean
first-dark in CurvedLongDepleted: loud **1,035.0** against quiet
**1,783.5** (never-dark counted as 2,400). Advertisement first, on
any ground, at any length surveyed.

**The law, nineteenth watch:** the disk's immortality was a
photograph, not a law — but the negative was unevenly exposed. A
longer season spends the cities that are *visited*, in the order of
their remaining stock, and collects its bills in exactly the order
the short season left them (thinnest first, at 1,445). What no
length of season has yet spent is the city nobody reaches: city 8
keeps 17.17 of its stock after 2,400 ticks not because the mouth is
slow there but because the journeys do not arrive — 783 visits, the
emptiest apron on either ground. *On curved ground, immortality is
not priced in stock or in appetite; it is priced in arrivals, and
the truly ignored are immortal at any season length yet surveyed.*
