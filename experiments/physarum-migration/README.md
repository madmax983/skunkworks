# physarum-migration 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-pastseason × the past window
itself, sliced (one ground, one window, eight dates)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-pastseason → the unmarked
hour inside ticks 7,200..9,600 where the haunting changed
tenants. The parent priced the window whole and found a second
life and a second death inside it; the bearing runs from the
window's total to the tick the collection moved. The farthest
corner of this map is, for the fifth time, a *when*, not a
*where* — but this time the when is inside the window, not past
it.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-pastseason` — the Cartographer's twenty-second
survey, which carried `physarum-lastseason`'s machinery whole
and extended the season to 9,600 ticks — closed with a tenure,
not a settlement. In CurvedPastDepleted, quiet city 8 received
**7,346 ghost visits in the past window** (ticks 7,200..9,600)
— more than twice its whole gated lifetime's 3,097, the largest
window count of any city by a factor of four — and then, in the
final 300 ticks, received **none**. The season's last sound
belonged to loud city 1 (383 in the final 300), the disk's
first fall at tick 591, with loud city 5 at 373 and quiet
city 6 at 15. The parent's law, its twenty-second watch: *a
ghost is not an inheritance; it is a lease, and the field
renews it where it pleases.* Its closing seed asks for the
lease's dates, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim:
*the migrating voice, dated — city 8's 7,346 past-window ghost
visits all arrived before tick 9,300 and its final-300 count
is 0: a finer instrument (ghost visits per city per 300-tick
slice across the past window) could date the migration — did
city 8's collection fade gradually or break at a tick, and
did city 1's apron rise as city 8's fell, or only after?*

## The novel trait

Pastseason's machinery is carried whole — agents as points in
the unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on the
projection with the void drinking at the rim, the mold's
boundary rule, the opposed channels stone for stone (loud
stamp 1.0 eaten at 0.004, quiet stamp 0.40 eaten at 0.010),
the same seed, the same nine cities at the same disk
addresses, the same fifteen rooms at the same five lengths —
and exactly one instrument is added:

- **The slice ledger.** In every room long enough to reach
  tick 7,200, cumulative per-city ghost visits are
  snapshotted at ticks 7,200, 7,500, 7,800, 8,100, 8,400,
  8,700, 9,000, 9,300 and 9,600. The eight differences are
  the past window's slices, printed per city in
  CurvedPastDepleted's ledger. The instrument is exact by
  construction: the slices sum, per city, to the window
  counts the parent priced by difference across rooms, and
  the eighth slice *is* the final-300 ledger, read by a
  second path. Nothing about the world changes; only the
  dating improves.

The 1,200-, 2,400-, 4,800- and 7,200-tick rooms stand as
controls and must reproduce their parents' pinned ledgers
exact, and the 9,600 rooms must reproduce pastseason's.

concealed it: the tenure did not move, it thinned, and the
parent's lease law needs a slower verb. If city 1's rise
comes only in slice 7, after city 8 is already silent, the
tenure was not passed but vacated and re-let, and there is a
gap in the haunting's occupancy no window total could show.
Either result stands in the ledger below, in those terms.

**P2 CRACKED — neither a burst nor a rain: a plateau.** City
8's largest slice (slice 4, **1,966**) holds **26.8%** of its
7,346 window total, below the declared 30%. But the profile
is not the falsification's flat rain either — no slice is
near the 12.5% an even tenure would give. City 8 collects on
a plateau: 1,469 / 1,654 / 1,650 / 1,966 across slices 1–4,
four slices within a third of each other, holding roughly
five-sixths of its whole window take (6,739 of 7,346) — and
then the plateau ends.

**P3 HELD — and the break is earlier and sharper than the
parent could see.** City 8's slice 6 is **17** (positive, as
declared) and its slice 7 is exactly **0**. But the dated
ledger shows the collection did not run to 9,300 and stop:
it broke between slice 4 and slice 5 — **1,966 → 212**, an
89% fall in a single slice, at tick **8,700** — then tailed
212 → 17 → 0 across two more slices. The parent's "all before
tick 9,300" was true and loose by 600 ticks: the tenure's
working life ended at 8,700, and what followed was its echo.

**P4 HELD — the successor's rise is late, and it is two
bursts, not a gathering.** City 1's largest slice is slice 7
(**383**), as declared. Its whole window profile is 0 / 0 /
0 / 0 / **333** / 0 / 0 / **383**: a single burst at slice 4 —
the very slice of city 8's plateau peak — then silence for
two slices, then the final burst that takes the last sound.
The first-fallen did not gather the tenure gradually; it
visited it once, at its height, and returned for it at the
end.

**P5 CRACKED — the lease does not open in city 8's name.**
Slice 0's argmax is **city 5 (541)** against city 8's 378:
at the window's opening the constant tenant, not the last
survivor, collects most. City 8 takes the argmax only from
slice 1 and holds it through slice 5. City 5 is the only
city present in **every** slice (541 / 125 / 92 / 299 / 8 /
193 / 172 / 373) — never the window's story, never absent
from it.

**P6 HELD — the tenure passes loud.** Slice 7's two largest
counts are city 1 (383) and city 5 (373), both loud, with
city 6's 15 the only other voice.

**The migration, dated:** the haunting's occupancy inside
the past window runs city 5 (slice 0) → city 8's plateau
(slices 1–5, peaking at slice 4) → city 5 again (slice 6)
→ cities 1 and 5 (slice 7). There is no gap slice — every
slice has a collector — and no single handover: city 8's
break at 8,700 precedes city 1's final rise by two full
slices, during which city 5, the tenant that never left,
holds the map. The falsification's second clause is half
met: city 1's *decisive* rise does come after city 8's
silence, but its slice-4 burst shows the apron was already
reaching the residue while city 8 collected at its peak.

**The law, twenty-third watch:** *a ghost's tenure is a
plateau with a cliff, not a slope and not a spike: it holds
near its height for as long as the field feeds that apron,
then breaks within a single slice — here 1,966 to 212 at
tick 8,700 — and tails to nothing across two more. And the
succession is not a passing of one lease: the next loud
voice tried the apron once at the plateau's own peak, two
slices before the break, and the tenant who held the map in
the interval was the one who had never left it. Date a
migration and you find it was three events wearing one
name: a rehearsal, a break, and a return.*
