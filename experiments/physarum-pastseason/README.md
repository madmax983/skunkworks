# physarum-pastseason 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️

**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-lastseason → the unmarked

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-lastseason` — the Cartographer's twenty-first survey,
which carried `physarum-farseason`'s machinery whole and extended
its season again — closed with a fall and a sound. The fall:
quiet city 8, the rim address that three lengths could not spend,
darkened at tick **5,204**, only 404 ticks into the last window,
because at tick 4,800 it became the disk's only lit address and a
sole stamp collects the whole map's appetite (the 729 visits its
remaining 7.29 required arrived in 404 ticks, above 1.8 per tick
against the far window's 0.41; its window counts run 783 → 988 →
1,326). The law, its twenty-first watch: **immortality is priced
in arrivals, and arrivals are priced in company — an address is
immortal exactly as long as it has neighbours whose stamps divide
the journeys.**

The sound is what the parent left open. The disk ended 9 of 9
dark, but not silent: in its final 300 ticks, ghost visits
totalled **428** and the last voice was not a solo — city 8 held
**230**, loud city 5 held **195**, quiet city 4 held **3**. And
the haunting's composition reversed: the loud share of ghost
visits, which had diluted for three lengths (100% → 43.0% →
41.6%), rose at the fourth to **43.9%**, because once nothing
remains alive to fall, the haunting's composition belongs to the
dead map's geometry, not to the order of its deaths. The parent's
closing seed asks whether that geometry is a settlement or a
snapshot, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim: *the
last duet, priced — in the final 300 ticks city 8 (230) and loud
city 5 (195) share the disk's last sound, with city 4 at 3: a
season past the end (9,600 ticks) — does the haunting's
composition keep drifting (the loud share reversed at the fourth
length, 41.6% to 43.9%), and does city 5's apron ever pass city 8
as the last voice, or is the duet's balance set by the fall order
for good?*

## The novel trait

Lastseason's machinery is carried whole — agents as points in
the unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion on the
projection with the void drinking at the rim, the mold's boundary
rule, the opposed channels stone for stone (loud stamp 1.0 eaten
at 0.004, quiet stamp 0.40 eaten at 0.010), the same seed, the
same nine cities at the same disk addresses — and the season is
extended once more, to 9,600 ticks, in three new rooms:

- **CurvedPastEternal** (disk, 9,600 ticks, stocks in shadow only)
  and **CurvedPastDepleted** (disk, 9,600 ticks, stock gating):
  a season past the end — the depleted room's past window
  (ticks 7,200..9,600) contains no lit address at all, so every
  visit in it is a ghost visit, and the haunting can be priced
  with nothing left alive to confuse the ledger.
- **OpposedPastDepleted** (flat, 9,600 ticks) stands beside them
  as the control ground: flat has had no survivors since tick
  2,400, so its past room prices only how a haunting grows when
  there has been nothing left to spend for three whole seasons.

The 1,200-, 2,400-, 4,800- and 7,200-tick rooms stand as
controls and must reproduce their parents' pinned ledgers exact.
A past room's first 7,200 ticks are its last twin's whole season,
sample for sample — the truncation identity is printed in the
ledger and asserted in the tests, so the past rooms differ from
the last ones only in being allowed to continue. The ledger also
prices the past window directly: each city's visits and ghost
visits in ticks 7,200..9,600, taken as the past room's count
minus the last room's — exact, by that identity.

No instrument addition is declared this watch: the parent's
per-city final-300 ghost ledger is exactly the instrument this
seed prices, and it is read here at a fifth length, at both ends
of the past season (the last room's final 300 against the past
room's), so the duet's drift has a ledger to answer in.

**Falsification condition:** if city 5's apron passes city 8's
in the final 300 ticks at 9,600 — or a third voice rises into
the duet, or the loud share falls back below 41.6% — then the
duet's balance was never set by the fall order: a haunting's
composition is a live process with its own drift, priced by the
dead map's geometry hour by hour, and the last city to fall
holds its loudest voice only on loan from that geometry. If
instead the duet stands at both ends of a season in which
nothing was lit, the fall order is the haunting's constitution,
and the only question left is whether the sound itself ever
thins. Either result stands in the ledger below, in those terms.

## The ledger (headless, as found)

**B1 HELD, exact.** Flat OpposedDepleted reproduces the parent's
pinned ledger field for field: loud visits **73,231**, loud mean
first-dark **268.2**, ghost visits **53,800** (loud 41,917, a 77.9%

**B2 HELD, exact, at all four earlier lengths.** The curved
1,200 rooms reproduce `physarum-curved`'s pinned ledger field for
field: eternal loud **36,226** / quiet **4,712** (ratio 6.15),
mass **1,124.80** / coverage **1,908**; depleted **4 of 9** dark,
mass **921.05** / coverage **1,557**. The 2,400 rooms reproduce
`physarum-longseason`'s: CurvedLongDepleted **7 of 9** dark, mass
**940.62** / coverage **1,540**, stock left 20.48, city 8 lit at
**17.17** on **783** visits, city 3 lit at **3.31**;
OpposedLongDepleted **9 of 9** dark. The 4,800 rooms reproduce
`physarum-farseason`'s: CurvedFarDepleted **8 of 9** dark, mass
**452.64** / coverage **733**, stock left **7.29** — every unit of
it city 8's — ghost visits 24,703 at 41.6% loud, city 3 dark at
**3,252**; OpposedFarDepleted **9 of 9** dark. The 7,200 rooms
reproduce `physarum-lastseason`'s: CurvedLastDepleted **9 of 9**
dark, mass **509.72** / coverage **831**, ghost visits **27,306**
at 43.9% loud, city 8 dark at **5,204**, final-300 duet 230 / 195
/ 3; OpposedLastDepleted **9 of 9** dark. All truncation
identities print **true**, at all four steps of the ladder.

**P1 HELD — length is the only difference, a fourth time.** Both
new truncation identities print true: the past rooms' first
7,200 ticks are the last seasons sample for sample, mass series
and first_dark alike, on both grounds. The past window printed
in the ledger is therefore priced exact, by difference.

**P2 CRACKED — the duet does not persist; a new voice takes it.**
In CurvedPastDepleted's final 300 ticks, ghost visits total
**771**, and the two largest per-city counts are **loud city 1
at 383** and **loud city 5 at 373** — city 8, half of the
parent's duet, receives **none at all**. A third voice also
clears the prediction's ceiling: quiet city 6 holds **15**,
against the declared 10 (city 4, the parent's third chair, holds
0). The last sound at 9,600 is a different duet than the one at
7,200, and one of its singers was never in the old one.

**P3 CRACKED — the fall order does not set the balance.** City
8's final-300 ghost count at 9,600 is **0**, against city 5's
**373**: the last city to fall is not the haunting's loudest
voice a season past its fall — it is not a voice at all. And
the voice that replaced it is the opposite end of the fall
order: city 1 darkened at tick **591**, the first city ever to
fall on the disk, and ends the fifth length as the haunting's
loudest apron. Whatever sets the balance, it is not who fell
last; if anything, this ledger points at who fell *first*.

**P4 CRACKED — the drift reversed again.** The loud share of
ghost visits in CurvedPastDepleted is **38.9%** (loud 14,512 of
37,282) — below the last season's 43.9% and below even the far
season's 41.6%. The past window's own additions explain the
swing: of the **9,976** ghost visits the window added, only
2,519 were loud (25.3%), because the window's traffic belonged
to city 8's quiet apron (see P6). The loud share is not a drift
with a direction; it is a reading of whichever apron currently
collects, and the collector changed mid-window.

**P5 CRACKED — the haunting swelled.** The final 300 ticks hold
**771** ghost visits, above the declared 650 ceiling and 80%
above the last season's 428. The sound did not persist at its
scale; it grew, because the field it feeds on re-thickened
(final mass **657.11** / coverage **1,099**, against the last
season's 509.72 / 831): with no stamp left to spend, agent
deposits accumulate along the aprons the residue still favours,
and a thicker field draws more journeys past every apron on it.

**P6 CRACKED — the past window's traffic tripled instead of
thinning.** Total ghost visits grew by **9,976** (27,306 →
37,282), far above the declared 4,500 ceiling — and since every
visit in the past window is a ghost visit, total visits grew by
the same 9,976, against the last window's 3,333. The window's
own ledger is the strangest page in this survey: **city 8
received 7,346 ghost visits in ticks 7,200..9,600** — more than
twice its whole gated lifetime's 3,097, the largest window count
of any city by a factor of four (city 5 next at 1,803) — and
then, by the final 300 ticks, received **none**. City 8's ghost
had a second life, brighter than its first, and a second death
inside the same window; all 7,346 of its window visits arrived
before tick 9,300, after which the collection migrated to the
loud aprons of cities 1 and 5. A haunting's voices hold tenure,
not title.

**P7 HELD, by less than a unit — the shadow floor holds a fifth
length.** In CurvedPastEternal, city 8's shadow stock never
exhausts by 9,600 and ends at **10.84** — above the declared
10.0 floor, but by 0.84, where the last room's margin was 4.67
of stock above the same line's logic. Its shadow visits are
also rising: **1,416** total (383 in the past window, against
the eternal room's earlier ~0.14 per tick average — the window
ran at ~0.16). The floor still holds, but it is a floor with a
slope in it now, and the projection can be priced: at the past
window's rate the remaining 10.84 (1,084 visits at 0.010)
projects to a shadow fall near tick **16,400** — named here as
a seed, not a finding.

**P8 HELD — the order is a closed book.** Effective mean
first-dark in CurvedPastDepleted: loud **1,205.4** against quiet
**2,484.5** — the identical numbers the last season printed,
because every first_dark on the disk was written by tick 5,204
and the fifth length re-read them without revising one.
Advertisement first, on any ground, at any length surveyed —
five lengths running, and now with the book closed.

**The flat past room, for the record:** OpposedPastDepleted ends
9 of 9 dark, visits 660,597, ghost visits 619,268 (final-300
15,925), dark ticks 81,205, final mass **2,268.73** / coverage
**3,469** — on flat ground the haunting also grows past its last
room's scale, a full season after flat's last survivor fell.

**The law, twenty-second watch:** a season of 9,600 ticks spends
nothing — the disk was already dark — and yet everything in its
ledger moved: the haunting grew by 9,976 ghost visits, its loud
share swung from 43.9% back to 38.9%, and its last sound changed
singers entire. *A haunting's composition is not settled by the
fall order, first or last: it is a tenure held by whichever
apron the dead map's residue currently feeds, and the tenure
moves — the last city fallen spoke loudest, then fell silent a
second time inside one window, and the first city fallen ended
the season as its loudest voice. On curved ground, a ghost is
not an inheritance. It is a lease, and the field renews it where
it pleases.* What remains at the farthest corner of this map: a
field thicker than the day the last city fell (657.11 against
509.72), a shadow floor holding at 10.84 with a priced
projection, and a final 300 ticks in which the first voice ever
silenced on the disk is the one still speaking.
