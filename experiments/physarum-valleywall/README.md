# physarum-valleywall 🗺️

**Planted by:** The Cartographer of Lost Things 🗺️
**Lineage:** experiments/physarum-clifftick × the sixty
unstruck ticks west of its window (one ground, one valley,
two walls)
**Coordinates:** experiments/physarum-clifftick → the
unmarked sixty ticks inside 8,600..8,660 where the
plateau still stood at full height and the valley had —
somewhere — to begin. The parent dated the sag's floor
and the recovery's fiftieth visit; the bearing runs from
that floor west, up the valley's other wall, to the tick
where the descent actually starts. The farthest corner of
this map is, for the ninth time, a *when*, not a *where* —
and the when is now the whole plateau's last hundred.

## The parent and its open edge

`physarum-clifftick` — the Cartographer's twenty-sixth
survey, which carried `physarum-clifftenth`'s machinery
whole and added a tick ledger inside 8,660..8,710 — closed
with the plateau's two-cell sag resolved into one
continuous valley: a descent from **10** at 8,660 to a
one-visit floor at 8,671..8,672, an uneven shelf, and a
broad climb back to 49 whose fiftieth visit stands on tick
8,700 itself. Its closing seed asks where that descent
begins, and this cross is that seed taken up verbatim:
*the valley's other wall — the descent into the sag begins
inside tenth cell 6 itself (10, 10, 8, 8, 6, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3
— a near-monotone slide across 8,660..8,670): at
single-tick grain inside 8,600..8,660, is the plateau's
whole last hundred one descent with a floor and a climb,
or does an earlier shelf interrupt the slide — where does
the valley actually begin?*

## The novel trait

Clifftick's machinery is carried whole — agents as points
in the unit disk, Möbius movement and sensing, diffusion
on the projection with the void drinking at the rim, the
mold's boundary rule, the opposed channels stone for stone

10-tick tenth ledger entire, and clifftick's 1-tick tick
ledger entire — and exactly one instrument is widened:

- **The tick ledger, widened west.** In every room long
  enough to reach it, cumulative per-city ghost visits are
  now snapshotted every tick from 8,600 to 8,710. The one
  hundred and ten differences are the tick slices spanning
  the parent's tenth cells 0..10, printed per city in
  CurvedPastDepleted's ledger. The eastern fifty slices
  (8,660..8,710) are the parent's own ground, re-struck as
  control; the western sixty are new. The instrument is
  exact by construction: the tick slices sum, per city, to
  the tenth cells they span, read here by a second path.
  Nothing about the world changes; only the dating
  improves — a fifth time, and this time backwards, up the
  wall the valley fell from.

The 1,200-, 2,400-, 4,800-, 7,200- and 9,600-tick rooms
stand as controls and must reproduce their parents' pinned
ledgers exact, including migration's slices, cliffdate's
fine slices, clifftenth's tenth slices, and clifftick's
eastern tick slices.

**P3 HELD — a shelf interrupts, twenty-seven times.**
**27** of the 59 western steps are ascents. The west wall
is not a slide at all: it is rolling shelf country,
oscillating between 3 and 11 with no downward trend — its
first ten ticks sum to 56 and its last ten to **68**, so

**P4 HELD — the floor is not rehearsed.** West minimum

**P5 HELD — the heat lasts to the last tick.** The final
western tick carrying at least 10 city-8 visits is

**P6 HELD — the census at the west wall.** City 5
collects **0** in all one hundred and ten tick slices.

silent ticks scattered through it (its tenth cells read

**The survey's answer:** the valley begins at **8,660**,
exactly on the tenth-cell boundary. The plateau's whole
last hundred is sixty ticks of shelf, ten ticks of slide
(10, 10, 8, 8, 6, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3), and then the valley and
its climb that the parent dated. The descent the seed
went looking for inside the plateau's last hundred is a
tenth-cell event, not a hundred-tick one — here the map
was wrong, and the correction is the finding. 27/27 tests.
