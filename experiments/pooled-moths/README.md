## The Novel Trait

Every waning room expired **both pools together**. The 750 room's
16 burns were always read as a property of the lifetime; nobody
had asked whether they were a property of *which pool* was
expiring. This planting keeps the evidence room's packets,
re-judgement, aversion and acquittal rule verbatim, books caution
by source exactly as `waning-moths` does, and at lifetime **750**
expires exactly **one** pool by age while the other pool's units
persist forever:

- **Pooled-taught** — taught units expire 750 ticks after gain;
  earned units never expire. The hearsay test.
- **Pooled-earned** — earned units expire at 750; taught units
  never expire. The scars test.

Seventeen twin rooms, same seed: the fifteen parents, unchanged
(tests pin their ledgers exactly — including waning-1500 fed 43 /
burns 12, expired 42 taught / 20 earned; waning-750 fed 48 /
burns 16, checks 103, events 502, expired 58 / 27; waning-375
fed 53 / burns 11; waning-150 fed 52 / burns 11), plus the two
pooled rooms. The moths know only what the field delivers and
what it cost them; the pool a unit sits in is the keeper's
bookkeeping, never the moth's.

landing on the same journey.

## Falsification condition

This planting is wrong if, on another seed or cave, one pooled
room reproduces waning-750's danger alone (burns ≥ 15 while its
sibling sits ≤ 13) — that would name a carrier pool after all —
or if a room expiring both pools at 750 on a *staggered* clock
(one pool offset by half a lifetime) still burns 16, which
would acquit the coincidence and indict the lifetime itself.
