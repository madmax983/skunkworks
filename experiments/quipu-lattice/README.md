# Quipu Lattice 🧶💎

> "The structure of the crystal encodes the history of the swarm."

**Quipu Lattice** is a hybrid experiment visualizing a 3D data structure where the bonds between nodes are Incan Quipu cords.

## Lineage 🧬

*   **Parent A:** `experiments/chimera-lattice` (Crystallographic Intelligence, 3D TUI Rendering)
*   **Parent B:** `experiments/quipu-symphony` (Quipu Data Structures, Knot Logic)

## Concept 💡

In a standard crystal lattice, bonds are static forces. In a **Quipu Lattice**, every bond is a recording device. Agents traverse the lattice, and as they move between nodes, they tie knots on the bonds (Quipu cords).

*   **Nodes:** Lattice points (Simple Cubic, BCC, FCC).
*   **Bonds:** Quipu Cords holding integer values.
*   **Agents:** Mobile entities that weave the history of their traversal into the structure itself.

## Emergence 🦋

The "Strength" or "Weight" of the lattice connections is dynamic and historically determined. High-traffic paths become heavy with knots (visualized as `●`, `≡`, `∞`), creating a visual density map of the swarm's activity in 3D space.

## Controls 🎮

*   **Arrows:** Rotate Camera
*   **+/-:** Zoom In/Out
*   **Space:** Switch Lattice Type (SC, BCC, FCC)
*   **R:** Reset Simulation
*   **Q/Esc:** Quit
