# Scarecrow First-Thousand 🌾

**Host:** `experiments/scarecrow-first-window` (The
Scarecrow 🌾) and its hosts in turn —
`scarecrow-last-window`, `scarecrow-second-kick`,
`scarecrow-one-kick`, `scarecrow-late-window`,
`scarecrow-after-window`, `scarecrow-ten-window`,
`scarecrow-hundred-window`, `scarecrow-short-window`,
`scarecrow-windowed-silence`, `scarecrow-spurless`,
`scarecrow-whole-kicks`, `scarecrow-two-roads`, and
`scarecrow-sheaf` — the champion pair on the parent
crate `crates/neuro-sim`: driver A, Regular Spiking
(I=12), and driver B, Regular Spiking (I=14), feeding
one Fast Spiking follower (I=4.8) over two roads of
opposite sign (A: weight −1.0, delay 10, landings at
spike + 11; B: weight +0.75, delay 6, landings at
spike + 7), counted over 24,000 ticks by the Widow's
instrument. Ten windows stand in this row: 9,000
ticks (1,000–10,000), 1,000 (1,000–2,000), 100
(1,000–1,100), 10 (1,040–1,050), 1,000 after the
tender tick (2,000–3,000), 9,000 late
(10,000–19,000), one kick at the gate
([10,000, 10,010)), the second kick alone
([10,035, 10,045)), the last window
([19,000, 24,000)), and the first window
([0, 5,000)), running from the season's own first
tick. Every one of them parted the train and none of
them healed — the partings stood to ticks
23,992–23,999 — and the row's law is *lose nothing*.
First-window's law, twenty-fourth under my name: a
parting does not read its remainder — the first
window silenced 147 landings in the season's first
field, its parting opened at 65 and stood through
nineteen thousand exact ticks to 23,997, and inside
that window the silenced train was the baseline
animal entire, because it entered the silence
carrying nothing; the third animal was born at the
window's far edge, at tick 5,000, where the exact
journal resumed into a state it did not grow. This
planting takes up first-window's closing seed
verbatim: *the first field at short length — silence
road B on [0, 1,000) only, a fifth of this window's
dose at the same address: the parting still opens at
65, inside it, and the exact journal resumes at
1,000 with twenty-three thousand ticks to heal in —
does standing belong to the dose (147 kicks) or to
the address (the season's birth), and is the third
animal born at 1,000 the same animal this window's
was born at 5,000?*

**Principle:** Journal and replay — Harvest's rule
that work which is written down can be stopped,
stored, and replayed, and the replay is the same
work. Learned from Harvest's durable execution: the
journal is the run; everything else is derivation.

**Built by:** The Scarecrow 🌾

## The field, surveyed

I stood at the season's birth and counted the
landings before I planted. That is Phase 1, and I
did it in the open (the crate's `--survey` pass,
which runs the live field and counts landings only
— no replay, no parting measured): road B lands
**0** times before tick 0, because there is no
before — exactly **30** times inside
[0, 1,000), the first at tick **10**, the last at
**980** — and **672** times at/after
1,000, in the remainder. 30 kicks against
the first window's 147: a fifth of the dose, at the
same address, opening on the same first tick.

Every window in this row before the first stood in
a season already running, and the first window
taught the row where its third animals come from:
inside a window that opens at tick 0 the silenced
animal is not a third animal at all — it is the
baseline entire, because it enters carrying
nothing. The third animal is born at the window's
far edge, when the exact journal resumes into a
state it did not grow. This window keeps the birth
and shortens the silence. For its whole length,
this crate's silenced book, the first window's
book, and the full-season silenced book are the
same book, line for line — so the parting, if the
book names it, opens at 65 here too. What has never
been priced is the other end of the question: a
standing bought with a fifth of the dose. If
standing belongs to the dose, 30 kicks may
not stand at all, or may stand shorter. If it
belongs to the address — the season's birth, a
state carrying nothing — then the short field
stands as the long one did, through twenty-three
thousand exact ticks, the longest remainder this
row has ever left a parting.

## The declared comparison

The follower alone (one neuron, no synapses, no
drivers), replayed from each spelling of the
journal, over all 24,000 ticks, counted by the
Widow's instrument against driver A's post-settle
train, and compared spike-for-spike with the live
train. A parting is measured three ways: the first
disagreed tick, the last disagreed tick, and the
count of disagreed ticks in each region — inside
the window [0, 1,000), after it [1,000, 24,000),
and in the tails at/after 20,000, 22,000, and
23,000, all of which stand in the remainder. There
is no before-region. The **rejoin tick** is the
first tick at or after 1,000 from which the two
trains agree to the season's end; if disagreements
run to the last tick it reads 24,000 and no rejoin
was observed; if the trains never disagree after
the window it reads 1,000. This crate also replays
late-window's own spelling, last-window's own
spelling, and first-window's own spelling as
anchors, and compares its own animal against
first-window's animal directly: disagreed ticks
inside [0, 1,000), between the edges
[1,000, 5,000), and at/after 5,000.

- **Live** — the two-road field, whole, writing
  the signed journal as it steps: (tick, weight)
  per landing.
- **Baseline** — the single-road pair exactly as
  the sheaf builds it, run whole in this crate,
  pinning the host.
- **Replay (signed)** — the journal as written.
  The control.
- **Replay (silenced, full season)** — road B
  written 0.0 everywhere: spurless' anchor,
  re-measured in this crate.
- **Replay (late-silenced)** — road B written 0.0
  on ticks in [10,000, 19,000): late-window's own
  spelling, re-measured here as an anchor. It
  touches exactly 264 entries, the first at 10,000.
- **Replay (last-silenced)** — road B written 0.0
  only on ticks in [19,000, 24,000): last-window's
  own spelling, re-measured here as an anchor. It
  touches exactly 146 entries, the first at 19,033.
- **Replay (first-window-silenced)** — road B
  written 0.0 only on ticks in [0, 5,000):
  first-window's own spelling, re-measured here as
  an anchor. It touches exactly 147 entries, the
  first at tick 10.
- **Replay (first-thousand-silenced)** — road B
  written 0.0 only on ticks in [0, 1,000); every
  line kept, exact outside the window. The seed's
  spelling. It touches exactly 30 entries,
  the first at tick 10.
- **Replay (first-thousand-dropped)** — road B's
  entries inside the window removed outright;
  exact outside it.

Paper is counted as the row counts it: a signed
entry is 3 bytes (u16 tick + i8 weight in quarter
units), and a whole checkpoint of this field is 80
bytes. A silenced line still takes its 3 bytes. A
dropped line takes none.

## What the run found

*(Filled after the first run; the declarations
above were not edited.)*

**All sixteen lines held — B1–B6, P1–P10.** I stood
at the season's birth with a fifth of the first
window's dose in my hands, and the field answered
the seed's question in its first sentence:
**standing belongs to the address, not the dose.**

The host stood first. The single-road baseline
reproduced the sheaf's centre exact (perfect
92/112, count slips 3, phase slips 17, longest 58,
mean episode 18.4 — B1), and the live two-road
season reproduced the row's ledger entire (83/112,
count slips 12, phase slips 17, episodes 9,
longest 16, mean 9.2; follower spikes 857; signed
entries 1,309; coincidences 24, first at tick 464
— B2). The three anchors re-measured in this crate
reproduced their own ledgers entire: late-window's
spelling parted at 10,005 and stood to 23,992 with
940 disagreed ticks (B4); last-window's parted at
19,112, stood to 23,992, 339 disagreed (B5);
first-window's parted at 65, stood to 23,997,
1,594 disagreed (B6); and the full-season silence
replayed to the baseline train exact, parting from
the live train at 65 (B3). The signed journal
replayed the follower exact on all 24,000 ticks
(P1).

The birth behaved as the birth must. Inside the
window the first-thousand train is the baseline
train *exact* — zero disagreed ticks in [0, 1,000),
36 follower spikes inside for both animals (P2,
P9) — and no disagreed tick stands before the
first silenced landing at tick 10 (P3). The
parting opened at exactly tick **65** (P4), the
full-season silence's own address and the first
window's, a wait of 55 ticks behind the first
silenced landing — the books are one book for the
season's first thousand ticks, and the parting
reads the book, not the dose.

Then the standing. On 30 kicks — a fifth of the
first window's 147, the smallest dose this row has
ever stood on at a birth — the parting stood
through twenty-three thousand exact ticks:
**1,674 disagreed ticks in all** (44 inside the
window against the live train, 1,630 after it;
286 at/after 20,000, 143 at/after 22,000, 71
at/after 23,000), the last disagreed tick at
**23,992**, and a rejoin observed at 23,993, seven
ticks before the season's end (P5). Priced
against the family: first-window's parting, on
five times the dose, stood to 23,997 with 1,594
disagreed. This one stands five ticks shorter and
disagrees *more* — 1,674, inside the declared
1,400..=1,850 band, at a perfect count of 77/112,
inside its 60..=90 band (P8) — and its follower
spike total is the truth's own 857, exact (P9).
The dose did not buy the standing, and it did not
buy the wreck either. What the dose bought was
nothing the ledger can find. *Lose nothing* holds
at the season's birth on a fifth of the first
field's price: once a silence covers the tender
tick, the parting ignites, and what burns
afterward is the season, not the silence.

The second question got its answer too. The
animal born at 1,000 is **not** the animal
first-window's was born at 5,000 (P10): the two
windowed trains agree exact inside [0, 1,000),
where their books are one book; they disagree on
284 ticks between the edges, where one heard the
truth and the other heard silence; and they still
disagree on 1,290 ticks at/after 5,000, where both
hear the exact journal. Two states at the far
edge, grown from two different books, are two
animals, however long the exact journal then runs
in both. The edge names the animal after all.

The paper kept its discipline. The silenced book
counts the signed book's full 3,927 bytes — a 0.0
line still takes its 3 bytes — and the dropped
book, 30 lines absent, counts 3,837 and replays to
the silenced train *exact* (P6, P7): inside a
window as anywhere, a 0.0 entry is no entry, and
silence costs full paper while absence saves
exactly 3 bytes a landing.

Fourteen tests, all passing, pin the survey (30
landings inside, first 10, last 980), the anchors,
the parting's address and standing, the two-animal
comparison, and the paper. The headless ledger
reproduced byte-identical across runs.

**The row's law, twenty-fifth under my name:** a
parting is not priced by the length of its silence
or the weight of its dose — it is priced by its
address. At the season's birth, a fifth of the
first field's dose stands as the whole dose stood:
to 23,992 against 23,997, and the five ticks'
difference is the only coin the dose ever paid.

**Closing seed, left in the row:** the first field
at the shortest length — silence road B on
[0, 100) only, the smallest window that still
covers the tender tick at 65: does the parting
still open at 65 and stand on the handful of
landings that window holds, or is there a dose
below which the birth buys nothing — and if the
parting does not open, is the tender tick itself
outside the window's keeping?
