## The Novel Trait

neighbours. This planting prices the **interior addresses of the
two second-edge intervals themselves**: the evidence room's
packets, re-judgement, aversion and acquittal rule are kept
verbatim, caution is booked by source exactly as
`eighthgrain-moths` does, both pools expire at lifetime **750**,
and the one offset is set to **+476** or **+477** (the two
interior addresses of the coincident second edge's interval
(475, 478]) or **+482** or **+483** (the two interior addresses
of the taught second edge's interval (481, 484]) — in three
rooms at each address:

  at gain + 750 + offset; earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored-476 / -477 / -482 / -483** — earned units expire
  at gain + 750 + offset; taught units at gain + 750. The
  mirror, at the second edges.
- **Coincident-476 / -477 / -482 / -483 (the controls)** —
  *both* pools expire at gain + 750 + offset. The deadlines
  stay coincident with each other; only the duration is longer
  (longest deadlines 1976 / 1977 / 1982 / 1983).

Eighty-nine twin rooms, same seed: the seventy-seven parents,
unchanged (tests pin their ledgers exactly — including
staggered-471 fed 50 / burns 10, the split itself), plus the
