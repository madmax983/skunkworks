## The Novel Trait

Every waning room so far expired its pools on deadlines that a
unit's gain tick alone determined: two units gained together —
one taught, one earned — died together. This planting keeps the
evidence room's packets, re-judgement, aversion and acquittal
rule verbatim, books caution by source exactly as `pooled-moths`
does, expires **both** pools at lifetime **750**, and adds exactly
one thing per new room: a **+375-tick offset** on an expiry
deadline.

- **Staggered** — taught units expire at gain + 750 + 375;
  earned units at gain + 750. Co-gained units expire 375 ticks
  apart; the two forgettings no longer land on the same journey.
- **Staggered-both (the control)** — *both* pools expire at
  gain + 750 + 375. The deadlines stay coincident with each
  other; only the duration is longer. This room separates
  staggering from mere longer life: if it also drops to ~12,
  duration explains the buyback and the coincidence claim
  cracks; if it returns toward 16 while staggered sits low,
  the coincidence itself is indicted.

Nineteen twin rooms, same seed: the seventeen parents, unchanged
(tests pin their ledgers exactly — including waning-750 fed 48 /
burns 16, expired 58 taught / 27 earned; pooled-taught 43 / 10,
expired 111 / 0; pooled-earned 54 / 12, expired 0 / 20), plus the
two staggered rooms. The moths know only what the field delivers
and what it cost them; offsets are the keeper's bookkeeping,
never the moth's.

The censuses, kept beside the burns as this lineage has learned

the mid-flight stranding the coincidence story predicted is
exactly where the staggered room is safest.

**The law:** before blaming a lifetime, stagger it. Two
forgettings that land together cost more than either costs
alone — but price the stagger against a coincident control at
the same deadline, or you will credit the offset for what the
extra ticks bought. A census is not a cause, and neither is a
deadline: what strands a moth is two deadlines arriving on the
same journey.

## Falsification condition

This planting is wrong if, on another seed or cave, the
staggered room burns at or above the coincident control at the
same longest deadline (staggered ≥ staggered-both) — that
would acquit the coincidence at the exact comparison built to
indict it — or if a room offsetting the *earned* pool instead
of the taught pool (the mirror stagger) lands far from 9,
which would make the buyback a property of *which* pool waits,
not of the waiting apart itself.
