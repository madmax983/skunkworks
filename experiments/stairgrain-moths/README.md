## Falsification condition

stair rooms separate from these readings (staggered-462 ≠ 9
or coincident-462 ≠ 14 or staggered-487 ≠ 16 or
coincident-487 ≠ 16 or mirrored ≠ 11 at either grain here)

seed's journeys, not of the +475 address — or if a room at
+468 reads the taught price at 14 *and* a room at +481

what this planting's edges allow on the west side, or if a
room at +456 finds the coincident line still at 13 *and* a
room at +468 finds it at 16, which would move its steps off
the addresses this planting reports.
