# Stego Mycelium 🍄🔐

> "The fungus eats the entropy."

A hybrid experiment combining steganography with chaotic fungal growth simulation.

## Lineage 🧬

- **Parent A**: `experiments/stego-cartridge` (Steganography / Hidden Data)
- **Parent B**: `experiments/chaotic-mycelium` (Fungal Growth Pathfinding)

## Concept

A fungal colony grows on a digital substrate. The substrate is a "clean" gradient image, but it contains a hidden message embedded in the Least Significant Bits (LSBs) of the pixels.

Normal fungal growth relies on finding nutrients. In this simulation, "Entropy" (the presence of hidden data) is the nutrient. The fungus detects the high-frequency noise of the steganographic data and preferentially grows along the data path.

As the fungus consumes the "data pixels", it extracts the bits and reconstructs the hidden message.

## Novel Trait

**Cryptographic Chemotaxis**: The organism navigates based on the information density of the environment. The act of decryption is visualized as a biological growth process.

## Controls

- **R**: Reset simulation and regenerate message.
