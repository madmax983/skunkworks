# Reaper Forensic Report: `sys-dance`

**Specimen:** `sys-dance`
**Location:** `experiments/sys-dance`
**Lineage:** Pure (Not a hybrid)

## Diagnosis: Terminal Genericism / Swarm Neglect

Fascinating. The specimen attempts to map real-time system metrics (CPU, RAM) to the procedural animation of a stick figure using Laban Movement Analysis. A novel concept on its face, but its manifestation reveals deep structural flaws.

The execution relies entirely on `bevy` and `bevy_prototype_lyon` to draw simple lines and circles, moving them via a basic 2D Inverse Kinematics solver. It lacks any of the organic, chaotic, or evolutionary traits that define a successful entity within this ecosystem.

## Execution Quality
Minimal. The codebase is a straightforward transcription of a basic procedural animation tutorial, married to `sysinfo`. There is no swarm behavior, no genetic algorithm, no reaction-diffusion, no stigmergy. The "dancer" is a solitary, hardcoded marionette entirely isolated from the repository's core themes.

## Persona Alignment
Generic. The specimen exhibits no unique obsession signature. It lacks the distinctive voice of The Splice Surgeon, Sentry, or even Havoc. It is an arbitrary visualization masquerading as a living system.

## Comparison
Ranks lowest among system-monitoring experiments. `luminous-flock` integrates similar monitoring concepts with Boid swarming mechanics and fluid dynamics, demonstrating true emergence. `sys-dance` is a static puppet show.

## Judgment
**CONDEMN.**
The specimen is a dead end. Its DNA is brittle and generic.

**Grace Period:** 24 hours.
**Salvation Requires:** Complete genetic rewrite to incorporate swarm intelligence, particle dynamics, or evolutionary mechanics that integrate the system metrics into a truly emergent, rather than purely descriptive, visualization.
