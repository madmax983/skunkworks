## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-wide-stagger`: no
field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each
moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim
is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and
without a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths
sample the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96
ticks) and judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at
fixed delivery probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and
packets 0.30). Caution is booked by source in kind: caution
from a moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages
is **earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet
is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping
that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit remains,
the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as the principal
books it).

Twenty-seven twin rooms, same seed. The first twenty-four
are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned
exact by tests — including this stage's staggered fed 54 /
burns 11, expired 48 taught / 15 earned; staggered-both fed
54 / burns 11, expired 48 / 10; mirrored fed 55 / burns 12,
expired 62 / 11; staggered-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired
11 / 16; mirrored-wide fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 9;
coincident-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 7). The
three new rooms run this stage's waning-750 room
**verbatim**, differing only in the offset, set to +450
owner-ticks:

- **StaggeredStep** — taught units expire at gain + 750 +
  450 owner-ticks; earned units at gain + 750.
- **MirroredStep** — earned units expire at gain + 750 +
  450 owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror,
  at the step.
- **CoincidentStep** — BOTH deadlines offset +450, so the
  deadlines stay coincident at the longest deadline (1950
  owner-ticks); only duration changes. The control, as the
  principal planted it.

The stage translation, named in the open: the principal's
clock is the cave's shared tick, struck continuously. A
queue is sequential, so each unit here carries its gain
tick on its **owner's personal clock** — the ticks that
moth's own turns have consumed, running across dawns and
never reset — and expiry is discovered only when its owner
next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at each
dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in these
rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was
known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in
**claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a unit's
age is observed only at claim tops — so +450 is exactly
**one step** here, the first offset this stage can barely
see. Its earlier stagger performances frame the question.
At +375 both staggers **collapsed** below resolution:
staggered read its coincident control's headline entire
(fed 54, burns 11, checks 100, events 695, expired taught
48 in both) and differed only in the earned census (15
against 10); mirrored collapsed onto **waning-750 itself**
(fed 55, burns 12, checks 97, events 671 exact), differing
only in the earned census (11 against 16) and a single
acquittal. At +750 the stage separated exactly one thing:
staggered-wide and coincident-wide rose together to burns
**15**, three above waning-750 — widening the taught wait
past a leg **taxed** the pool the room spends first — while
mirrored-wide read waning-750's headline for the third time
in its family, its earned census shrinking 16 → 11 → 9 as
the offset grew. So the step role arrives with the
sharpest question this family has asked: *does the
separation begin at the step, or only past it?* If the
step rooms reproduce their +375 parents here as they did
in the cave, the shelf extends one full step on a stage
whose eye is the step itself, and separation lives
somewhere between one step and two. If they separate —
toward the wide rooms, or to readings of their own — the
step is this stage's resolution limit, and what the cave
calls a shelf the queue calls a boundary.

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *from +375 through the claim-step
itself the ledger does not move at all: the step rooms
reproduce their +375 parents, the optimum is a shelf, and
the turn lives past the step* — is falsified on this stage
room by room: a step room in which no packet is ever
witnessed, or in which either pool never expires, tests
nothing; if coincident-step burns **below** either step
stagger, coincident deadlines at the longest deadline beat
a stagger set at exactly one step, and the shelf is
acquitted at the exact comparison built to indict it; if
the step mirror gap lands far from the step taught stagger
(outside the declared band of 3), the gap did not hold here
in any direction the cave would recognize; and if the step
rooms separate from their +375 parents in the headline —
staggered-step ≠ staggered, or mirrored-step ≠ waning-750's
headline — the cave's shelf was a property of the cave's
continuous clock, not of the offset, and the ledger must
say so in those words. A collapse at +450, on a stage whose
eye is one step wide, is the shelf performed at its own
resolution limit; a separation at +450 is the boundary
found.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test —
an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim.
(Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets are as
declared, expiry fires in both pools in each step room,
and no dawn tax fires there.)

distance between the pool a room spends first and the
pool it spends last, and that distance survives every
swap: it is the one quantity in this family that belongs
to the booking, not to the stage.*
