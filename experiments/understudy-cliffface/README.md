## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-claimstep`, carried through `understudy-shelfedge`: no field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30). Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as the principal books it).

Thirty-three twin rooms, same seed. The first thirty are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned exact by tests — including this stage's staggered fed 54 / burns 11, expired 48 taught / 15 earned; staggered-both fed 54 / burns 11, expired 48 / 10; mirrored fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 11; staggered-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 15; coincident-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 10; staggered-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 16; mirrored-wide fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 9; coincident-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 7; staggered-edge fed 54 / burns 10, expired 21 / 15; mirrored-edge fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 10; coincident-edge fed 54 / burns 10, expired 21 / 10). The three new rooms run this stage's waning-750 room **verbatim**, differing only in the offset, set to +525 owner-ticks:

- **StaggeredFace** — taught units expire at gain + 750 + 525 owner-ticks; earned units at gain + 750.
- **MirroredFace** — earned units expire at gain + 750 + 525 owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, at the face.

The stage translation, named in the open: the principal's clock is the cave's shared tick, struck continuously. A queue is sequential, so each unit here carries its gain tick on its **owner's personal clock** — the ticks that moth's own turns have consumed, running across dawns and never reset — and expiry is discovered only when its owner next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at each dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in these rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in **claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a unit's age is observed only at claim tops — so +525 is between one step and two, inside the same interval the edge performance already priced: at +600 the taught side read the step's price entire (burns 10, sharing staggered-step's headline field for field), differing only in acquittals and in the taught census, which fell 35 → 21 between the step and the edge while no burn followed it. The face role therefore asks the sharpest version of the edge question at a finer address: *at +525, does the taught side hold the step price again, post this stage's first interior price, or move only in its census — and if the census moves, where between the step's 35 and the edge's 21 does the face's census stand?*
