## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-claimstep`, carried through `understudy-cliffface`: no field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30). Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as the principal books it).

Thirty-nine twin rooms, same seed. The first thirty-three are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned exact by tests — including this stage's staggered fed 54 / burns 11, expired 48 taught / 15 earned; staggered-both fed 54 / burns 11, expired 48 / 10; mirrored fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 11; staggered-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 15; coincident-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 10; staggered-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 16; mirrored-wide fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 9; coincident-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 7; staggered-edge fed 54 / burns 10, expired 21 / 15; mirrored-edge fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 10; coincident-edge fed 54 / burns 10, expired 21 / 10; staggered-face fed 54 / burns 10, expired 32 / 15; mirrored-face fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 11; coincident-face fed 54 / burns 10, expired 32 / 10). The stage translation, named in the open: the principal's clock is the cave's shared tick, struck continuously. A queue is sequential, so each unit here carries its gain tick on its **owner's personal clock** — the ticks that moth's own turns have consumed, running across dawns and never reset — and expiry is discovered only when its owner next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at each dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in these rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in **claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a unit's age is observed only at claim tops — so +475 and +500 both stand less than one claim-step past the step's own address (+450), deep inside the same interval the face performance already priced at 10 entire. The cave found its stair by bringing a *finer* lens to a continuous clock; this stage's lens cannot get finer than a claim. The grain role therefore asks the sharpest version of the face question at two nearer addresses: *at +475 and at +500, does the taught side hold the step price again, post this stage's first interior price, or move only in its census — and if the census moves, do the two grains' censuses separate at all inside the interval's first half, where the step reads 35 and the face reads 32?*

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *the fall whole at 75-tick grain is a stair at 25-tick grain: an intermediate price stands at +475, the bottom is already reached at +500, and both falling lines share both steps, while the earned price crosses the whole interval without stepping at all* — is falsified on this stage room by room: a grain room in which no packet is ever witnessed, or in which either pool never expires, tests nothing; if a coincident grain room burns **below** either grain stagger, coincident deadlines at that longest deadline beat a stagger inside the interval's first half, and the grain is acquitted at the exact comparison built to indict it; if a mirror gap lands far from its taught stagger (outside the declared band of 3), the gap did not hold here in any direction either stage would recognize; and if staggered-475 and staggered-500 both read exactly the step's, face's and edge's 10, this stage prices its offsets in whole steps only, and the ledger must say so in those words — an interior landing (12..=14) is the first mid-step price this stage has ever read, a tax landing (15) would mean the five-burn tax arrives inside the interval's first half here, where the face and the edge said it did not, and a disagreement between the two grains (P7) would mean the stage *can* resolve 25 owner-ticks after all, somewhere below the price. A hold, an interior, a tax, a census-only move, or a grain that separates — the grains are reported as found.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test — an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim. (Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets are as declared, expiry fires in both pools in each grain room, and no dawn tax fires there.)
