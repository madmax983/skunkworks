## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-claimstep`, carried through `understudy-ninegrain`: no field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30). Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as the principal books it).
