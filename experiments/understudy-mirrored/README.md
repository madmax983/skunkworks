## The Principal and the Role

`mirrored-moths` took up `staggered-moths`' closing
falsification condition verbatim — *a room offsetting the
earned pool instead of the taught pool (the mirror stagger)
landing far from 9 would make the buyback a property of which
pool waits, not of the waiting apart itself* — and answered
it in the FDTD cave, caution booked by source on a
dawn-crossing global clock. Both pools expire at lifetime 750;
the one offset moved from the taught pool's deadline to the
earned pool's. Its ledger: **mirrored** (earned deadline
+375) fed 46, burns **11**, checks 129, events 662 (269
irregular / 393 steady), learners 18, acquittals 162, expired
taught **99** / earned **12** — against **staggered** (taught
+375) fed 50, burns **9**, expired 55 / 15;
**staggered-both** (the coincident control — both deadlines
+375, so only duration changes) fed 45, burns **13**, expired
58 / 19; and waning-750's fed 48, burns **16**, expired
58 / 27. All six predictions **held** (P2 exact, asserted by
test: offsets taught 0 / earned 375, the mirror image of
staggered's 375 / 0). The four-room ordering is complete and
strict: **staggered 9 < mirrored 11 < coincident-late 13 <
coincident-750 16** — a stagger is symmetric in kind and
asymmetric in price: the mirror gap is exactly 2 burns
against a coincidence gap of 4, and mirrored expires far more
taught units than staggered (99 against 55) yet burns only 2
more. A scar that waits protects nearly like a lesson that
waits; *nearly* is the measured word.

The role, extracted precisely: *a both-pools waning room at
one lifetime, re-performed with the earned pool's deadline
offset by half the lifetime instead of the taught pool's,
priced against the taught stagger and against a coincident
control at the same longest deadline — on fed, burns, checks,
events in both kinds, first learners, acquittals, and the two
expiry counts — and on whether the principal's mirror gap
survives the swap of machinery in any register.*

## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-staggered`: no field, no
grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a
claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin
flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a field
brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's
next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge
CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery
probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30). Caution
is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's own burns
and from delivered burn messages is **earned**; caution from
re-judging an evidence packet is **taught** (an acquittal
spends taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it —
if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent,
exactly as the principal books it).

Twenty-one twin rooms, same seed. The first twenty are the
inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned exact by
tests — including this stage's staggered fed 54 / burns 11,
expired 48 taught / 15 earned, and staggered-both fed 54 /
burns 11, expired 48 / 10). The one new room runs this stage's
waning-750 room **verbatim**, differing only in which pool's
deadline carries the offset:

- **Mirrored** — earned units expire at gain + 750 + 375
  owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. On paper the two
  forgettings land 375 apart, exactly as in staggered; only
  *which* forgetting waits has changed.

`staggered-both` already serves as the coincident control at
the longest deadline (1125) for both staggers on this stage,
as it did in the cave, so no second control is planted. The
stage translation, named in the open: the principal's clock
is the cave's shared tick, struck continuously. A queue is
sequential, so each unit here carries its gain tick on its
**owner's personal clock** — the ticks that moth's own turns
have consumed, running across dawns and never reset — and
expiry is discovered only when its owner next moves: at the
top of its turn's claims, and at each dawn on its personal
clock. There is no dawn tax in this room. No RNG is drawn by
expiry.

Two stage facts shape the whole performance, and both were
known before the curtain. First, this stage has already
**collapsed** the principal's predecessor: in the cave,
staggered beat its coincident control strictly (9 < 13); on
this stage staggered and staggered-both read **one headline
table** — fed 54, burns 11, checks 100, events 695, learners
11, acquittals 162, expired taught 48 in both — and differed
only where no headline reads, the earned expiry census (15
against 10) and a single witness learner. Priced on this
stage: duration 1 burn, the stagger 0 — because, second, this
stage's time moves in **claim-sized steps of roughly 450
owner-ticks**: a unit's age is observed only at claim tops,
and the principal's offset is **375 ticks — below one
claim-step**. So the mirror arrives on this stage with its
headline already suspect: the cave priced the mirror gap at
2 burns, a currency this stage may not be able to mint at
all. The performance may collapse — mirrored, staggered, and
coincident reading one table — not because the mirror law is
false, but because *this stage cannot see a 375-tick gap, on
either pool*. The ledger must say which register, if any, the
mirror registers in: a crack with an address (the step, and
the census) is worth more than a hold with none.

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *a stagger is symmetric in kind and
asymmetric in price: which forgetting waits is worth 2 burns,
and the waiting apart is worth 4 against coincidence in both
directions* — is falsified on this stage room by room: a
mirrored room in which no packet is ever witnessed, or in
which either pool never expires, tests nothing; if mirrored
burns **above** staggered-both, coincident deadlines at the
same longest deadline beat the mirror stagger and the mirror
is acquitted at the exact comparison built to indict it; if
mirrored lands far from this stage's staggered (outside
8..=14), the mirror gap on this stage is not the cave's 2
burns in any direction; if mirrored, staggered, and
staggered-both land on one table *censuses included*, the
mirror is below this stage's resolution entire and the law
survives only untested — the ledger must say which, and must
not call a collapse a confirmation.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test —
an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim.
(Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets are as
declared, expiry fires in both pools in the mirrored room,
and no dawn tax fires there.)

the coincident-750 room. Every headline field is
waning-750's (fed 55, burns 12, checks 97), every event too

the address the README declared before the run, plus one the
stage taught while performing: the offset is 375 ticks,
below this stage's ~450-tick claim-step, *and* it was laid
on the pool this stage spends **last**. Acquittals spend
taught caution first and touch earned units only when no
taught unit remains — so extending the taught deadline (the
stagger) reached night 3's behaviour (events 275 against
251, one burn bought), while extending the earned deadline
(the mirror) reached almost nothing that moves: the earned
pool is the reserve, and a reserve that waits longer is
still, on most nights, just waiting. The stage ordering

**staggered 9 < mirrored 11 < coincident-late 13 <

waits is worth something everywhere; what it is worth, and
whether it can outrun a control that secretly carries the
other stagger, is a property of the stage's spending order,
not of the waiting apart.*
