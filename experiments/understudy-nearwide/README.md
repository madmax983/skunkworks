## The Principal and the Role

`understudy-facegrain` closed with a seed that had already stood through two earlier performances, verbatim in substance: *the stagger at **+675** on the queue stage — midway between the edge and the wide offset: if the tax arrives there at all, it arrives inside the last 150 ticks — does staggered-675 read 10, 15, or the first interior price this stage has ever posted, and does its taught census (35 at the step and at +475, 33 at +500, 32 at the face, 21 at the edge, 11 at the wide) fall with the price or ahead of it?*

The role, extracted precisely: *the both-pools waning room at lifetime 750, performed with the stagger set to +675 owner-ticks in both directions beside a coincident control at the same longest deadline (2175) — priced on fed, burns, checks, events in both kinds, first learners, acquittals, and the two expiry counts — against this stage's pinned row, in which the taught price has moved exactly once in six measured addresses (10 at +450, +475, +500, +525 and +600; 15 at +750, the five-burn tax), while the taught census fell the whole way across (35, 35, 33, 32, 21, toward the wide room's 11) and the acquittals drifted 169, 169, 171, 172, 175 before dropping to the wide room's 141. +675 is the midpoint of the last interval — the one address where "the tax arrives inside the last 150 ticks" can be halved.*

## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-claimstep`, carried through `understudy-facegrain`: no field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30). Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as the principal books it).

Forty-two twin rooms, same seed. The first thirty-nine are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned exact by tests — including this stage's staggered-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 taught / 15 earned; staggered-475 fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 15, acquittals 169 — the step room entire; staggered-500 fed 54 / burns 10, expired 33 / 15, acquittals 171; staggered-face fed 54 / burns 10, expired 32 / 15; staggered-edge fed 54 / burns 10, expired 21 / 15; staggered-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 16; mirrored rooms burns 12 at every width; coincident rooms following their taught staggers entire at every width). The three new rooms run this stage's waning-750 room **verbatim**, differing only in the offset, set to +675 owner-ticks:

- **Staggered675** — taught units expire at gain + 750 + 675 owner-ticks; earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored675** — earned units expire at gain + 750 + 675 owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, near the wide offset.
- **Coincident675** — BOTH deadlines offset +675, so the deadlines stay coincident at the longest deadline (2175 owner-ticks); only duration changes. The control, as the principal planted it.

The stage translation, named in the open: the cave's clock is shared and continuous. A queue is sequential, so each unit here carries its gain tick on its **owner's personal clock** — the ticks that moth's own turns have consumed, running across dawns and never reset — and expiry is discovered only when its owner next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at each dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in these rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in **claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a unit's age is observed only at claim tops — so every offset this stage has measured (+375 through +750) has been priced in whole claim-steps or not at all, and the only price change in the row stands somewhere inside the last interval, where the census has already been falling for four addresses. The nearwide question is therefore the sharpest version of the stage's standing question: *at the last interval's midpoint, has the tax arrived, is there an interior price at last — or does the census finish its fall ahead of a price that waits for the final 75 ticks?*

## Falsification Condition

The stage's standing claim — *on the queue stage an offset is priced in whole claim-sized steps: the taught price holds at 10 from the claim-step through the edge and is taxed only at the wide offset, while the bookkeeping (the taught census, the acquittals) resolves at finer grain than any price* — is falsified at this address room by room: a new room in which no packet is ever witnessed, or in which either pool never expires, tests nothing; if staggered-675 burns **12..=14**, an interior price exists on this stage after all, inside its last interval, and the whole-steps claim falls at its final address; if it burns **15**, the tax is not a last-75-ticks event — it has arrived by the interval's midpoint, and the census question is moot in the direction of "with"; if its taught census reads **21**, the census does not fall ahead anywhere — it waits with the price and the facegrain reading (census resolving inside intervals the price cannot) was a first-half accident; if a coincident room burns **below** either stagger, coincident deadlines at 2175 beat a stagger in the last interval, and the nearwide rooms are acquitted at the exact comparison built to indict them. A hold, an interior, a tax, a census that waits, or a census that arrives entire — the last interval is reported as found.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test — an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim. (Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets are as declared, expiry fires in both pools in each new room, and no dawn tax fires there.)
