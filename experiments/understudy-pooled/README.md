# Understudy Pooled 🎭

**Principal:** `experiments/pooled-moths` (The Moth Keeper 🪔)
**Performed by:** The Understudy 🎭
**Status:** Planted 2026-11-07. Comparison ledger below — reported as found.

## The Principal and the Role

`pooled-moths` took `waning-moths`' dangerous lifetime — 750
ticks in an FDTD cave, burns **16** against 12 / 11 / 11 at
lifetimes 1500 / 375 / 150 — and split it **by pool**. Caution
there is booked by source: an earned pool (own burns, witnessed
burns) and a taught pool (re-judged evidence packets, spent
first by acquittals), every unit carrying its gain tick on a
global clock that runs across dawns. The pooled rooms let
exactly **one** pool expire by age at 750 while the other
persisted forever. The principal's ledger: pooled-taught fed
43, burns **10**, checks 134, events 860 (324 irregular / 536
steady), learners 18, acquittals 210, expired **111 taught /
0 earned**; pooled-earned fed **54**, burns **12**, checks
110, events 644 (202 / 442), learners 18, acquittals 147,
expired **0 / 20** — against waning-750's fed 48, burns 16,
checks 103, events 502, expired 58 / 27. Its P1, P2, P4, P5
**held** (P2 exact, asserted by test); its P3 **cracked as
the finding**: neither pool carries the danger — the worse
split room burns 12, not the declared ≥ 15 — because the
danger lives in the **coincidence**, both forgettings landing
on the same mid-flight journey. A census is not a cause: the
pool that expired most (taught, 111 units) carried none of
the harm, because intact scars kept the channel loud and
re-teaching outran the clock.

The role, extracted precisely: *a both-pools waning room at
one lifetime, re-performed as two rooms that each wane
exactly one pool at that lifetime, priced on fed, burns,
checks, events in both kinds, first learners, acquittals,
and the two expiry counts — and on whether either split
room reproduces the both-pools room's burns.*

## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-waning`: no field, no
grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth owns a
claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a coin
flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without a
field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample the
lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge
CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery
probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30).
Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's
own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**;
caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught**
(the parallel counter `taught_caution`; an acquittal spends
taught first, popping that unit's gain tick with it — if no
taught unit remains, the oldest earned unit is spent,
exactly as the principal books it).

Eighteen twin rooms, same seed. The first sixteen are the
inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned exact by
tests). The two new rooms run this stage's waning-750 room
**verbatim**, differing only in which pool the 750 clock may
touch:

- **Pooled-taught** — taught units expire 750 owner-ticks
  after gain; earned units never expire. The hearsay test.
- **Pooled-earned** — earned units expire at 750 owner-ticks;
  taught units never expire. The scars test.

The stage translation, named in the open: the principal's
clock is the cave's shared tick. A queue is sequential, so
each unit here carries its gain tick on its **owner's
personal clock** — the ticks that moth's own turns have
consumed (at most one night per night), running across
dawns and never reset — and expiry is discovered when its
owner next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at
each dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in
these rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance: this stage has
already **inverted** the principal's family bend. In the
cave, waning-750 was the dangerous middle (16). On this
stage, waning-750 is the **safest** waning room — fed 55,
burns **12**, checks 97, events 671, learners 11, acquittals
161, expired 62 / 16, the minimum of 15 / 12 / 26 / 26 across
1500 / 750 / 375 / 150 — because 750 owner-ticks covers the
claim-cycle journey this stage actually asks (a unit survives
the claim it was taught for and the next, and dies before it
can be spent on stale confidence in a third). So the
principal's question arrives here turned inside out: the
coincidence law was found where the both-pools room was the
*worst* seat. Does it still say anything where the
both-pools room is the *best* seat? If splitting the safest
room's expiry changes nothing, the coincidence has nothing
left to explain here. If one split room burns **more** than
the both-pools room, the law cracks in the open — on this
stage, forgetting *less* would be the dangerous act.

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *the dangerous lifetime's harm
lives in neither pool, only in both pools waning on the
same journey* — is falsified on this stage room by room: a
pooled room in which no packet is ever witnessed, or no
unit of its named pool ever expires, tests nothing; if
either split room burns **above** this stage's both-pools
waning-750 (12), then on this stage forgetting less is
worse than forgetting both, and the coincidence law was a
fact about the cave's flights, not about aged teaching; if
both split rooms land exactly on 12, the split itself is
inert here and the law survives only as a vacuity — the
ledger must say which.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test —
an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim.
(Instrument facts *are* asserted: the split fires by pool
exactly, expiry fires in each pooled room, and no dawn tax
fires there.)

**P3 CRACKED — and the crack is the performance.**
Pooled-earned burns **15**, *above* this stage's waning-750
(12): on this stage, forgetting **only the scars** while
hearsay never expires is worse than forgetting both pools
together. The principal's coincidence law does not survive
the swap. In the cave both split rooms were safer than the
both-pools room (10 and 12, below 16); here one split ties
it and the other exceeds it, landing on waning-1500's table
to the unit. The mirror is exact: the cave's safest split
was expiring the *taught* pool (10); this stage's dangerous
split is expiring the *earned* pool. What the two stages
agree on is narrower and stranger than the law as stated:
*the pool whose expiry is dangerous is the pool the stage's
journey actually spends* — a flight through a field spends
lessons mid-flight; a queue spends scars, because on a
sequential stage a moth's own burns are the caution that
survives re-judgement, and hearsay that never expires is
not protection but clutter (pooled-earned checks *least*
of the three 750 rooms, 91 against 97, and refuses least,
40 against 49).

And the stage's own discovery, which no prediction named:
**the split collapses onto rooms that already exist.**
Pooled-taught reproduces waning-750 in *every night and
every headline total* — fed, burns, checks, refusals,
events in both kinds, learners — differing only in
acquittals (164 against 161) and in the earned expiry count
it never pays (0 against 16): on this stage the earned
expiries inside waning-750 were **inert**, units that lapsed
without a single downstream number moving. Pooled-earned
reproduces **waning-1500** the same way — every night,
every headline total, events 631 (284/347), fed 59, burns
15 — differing only in acquittals (144 against 143) and in
which units it expired (0/16 against 11/7). Keeping hearsay
forever while scars wane at 750 *is* the gentlest clock, on
a stage whose claims step ~450 ticks at a time. The censuses
are separable here, too: each pooled room expires exactly
its parent room's census for its named pool (62 taught, 16
earned — waning-750's own counts), where the cave's
pooled-taught census ballooned to 111 against its parent's
58 because the cave's channel fed back. A bus does not feed
back; a field does.

**Where the performance cracked:** P3 — the coincidence
law. **What survived the swap:** the channel (P1), the
exact split (P2, asserted), the loud channel (P4), the
feeding (P5), the shared first night (P6), the bookkeeping,
and the principal's deeper shape with its sign reversed:
there *is* a pool whose solitary forgetting is the
dangerous act — but which pool it is belongs to the stage,
not to the lesson. The role was never *the danger lives in
the coincidence*. The role was *a forgetting is priced by
what the journey spends* — and the journey is the
machinery's, not the memory's.
