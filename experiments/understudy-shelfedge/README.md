claim-step and the wide offset: where on the shelf's edge
does the price turn, and does the mirror gap close before,

caution booked by source on a dawn-crossing global clock.

to **+600** ticks, on the shelf's edge, in three rooms:

deadlines +600, coincident at the longest deadline 2100)
fed 46, burns **16**, checks 140, events 800 (267 / 533),

Three of six predictions held; **P3, P4, P5 cracked** —
staggered-edge burns 16, not the declared 10..=12: the
price does not turn toward the +750 dissolution (12) at
the midpoint, it falls clean past it to waning-750's own
unstaggered price; the mirror gap at the edge reads
**−4** (mirrored-edge holds the dissolution price while
its taught twin pays full — two prices passing in the
dark, not a closing); coincident-edge burns 16, outside
the declared 12..=13, so the cliff belongs to the longest
deadline's neighbourhood (2100), not to the stagger
arrangement. The thirteen-room cave ordering reads
**staggered 9 = staggered-step 9 < mirrored 11 =
mirrored-step 11 < staggered-wide 12 = mirrored-wide 12 =
coincident-wide 12 = mirrored-edge 12 < staggered-both
13 = coincident-step 13 < staggered-edge 16 =
coincident-edge 16 = waning-750 16** — the midpoint is
the worst address on the cave's road. The Keeper's
eighteenth law: *a shelf's edge is not a slope, and it is
not shared — measure the middle for each pool
separately.*

The role, extracted precisely: *the both-pools waning
room at one lifetime, re-performed with the stagger set
to +600 in both directions beside a coincident control

## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-claimstep`: no
field, no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight.
Each moth owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the
next claim is a coin flip — both lamps emit at mean
period 16, and without a field brightness has no
channel); cautious moths sample the lamp's next 5
intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and judge CV >
0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery
probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30).
Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a
moth's own burns and from delivered burn messages is
**earned**; caution from re-judging an evidence packet
is **taught** (an acquittal spends taught first, popping
that unit's gain tick with it — if no taught unit
remains, the oldest earned unit is spent, exactly as
the principal books it).

Thirty twin rooms, same seed. The first twenty-seven
are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned
exact by tests — including this stage's staggered fed
54 / burns 11, expired 48 taught / 15 earned;
staggered-both fed 54 / burns 11, expired 48 / 10;
mirrored fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 11;
staggered-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 15;
coincident-step fed 54 / burns 10, expired 35 / 10;
staggered-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 16;
mirrored-wide fed 55 / burns 12, expired 62 / 9;
coincident-wide fed 59 / burns 15, expired 11 / 7).
The three new rooms run this stage's waning-750 room
**verbatim**, differing only in the offset, set to +600
owner-ticks:

- **StaggeredEdge** — taught units expire at gain + 750 +
  600 owner-ticks; earned units at gain + 750.
- **MirroredEdge** — earned units expire at gain + 750 +
  600 owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. The
  mirror, at the edge.
- **CoincidentEdge** — BOTH deadlines offset +600, so
  the deadlines stay coincident at the longest deadline
  (2100 owner-ticks); only duration changes. The
  control, as the principal planted it.

The stage translation, named in the open: the
principal's clock is the cave's shared tick, struck
continuously. A queue is sequential, so each unit here
carries its gain tick on its **owner's personal clock**
— the ticks that moth's own turns have consumed,
running across dawns and never reset — and expiry is
discovered only when its owner next moves: at the top
of its turn's claims, and at each dawn on its personal
clock. There is no dawn tax in these rooms. No RNG is
drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was
known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in
**claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a
unit's age is observed only at claim tops — so +600 is
between one step and two, the first offset this stage
has ever been asked to price *inside* a step interval.
Its earlier stagger performances frame the question.
At +375 both staggers **collapsed** below resolution:
staggered read its coincident control's headline entire
(fed 54, burns 11, expired taught 48 in both) and
mirrored collapsed onto **waning-750 itself**. At +450,
exactly one step, the taught side **separated by
exactly one burn**: staggered-step burns **10**, one
below its +375 parent, its taught census falling 48 →
35, and coincident-step followed it down entire,
differing only in the earned census (10 against 15);
mirrored-step reproduced mirrored **entire** — the
earned offset has never once moved a headline field on
this stage. At +750 the stage separated exactly one
thing: staggered-wide and coincident-wide rose together
to burns **15**, five above the step — widening the
taught wait past a leg **taxed** the pool the room
spends first — while mirrored-wide read waning-750's
headline for the third time in its family. So the edge
role arrives with the sharpest question this family has
asked either stage: *does the taught side keep falling
from the step's 10, or turn up toward the wide tax of
15 — and where, between one step and two, does the
one-burn separation become the five-burn tax?*

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *at the midpoint the taught
price does not turn, it cliffs: staggered-edge pays
waning-750's full unstaggered price, the mirror gap
swings through zero to −4 because the two pools do not
share a turn, and even the coincident control is
charged full at the longest deadline* — is falsified on
this stage room by room: an edge room in which no
packet is ever witnessed, or in which either pool never
expires, tests nothing; if coincident-edge burns
**below** either edge stagger, coincident deadlines at
the longest deadline beat a stagger set between one
step and two, and the cliff is acquitted at the exact
comparison built to indict it; if the edge mirror gap
lands far from the edge taught stagger (outside the
declared band of 3), the gap did not hold here in any
direction either stage would recognize; and if
staggered-edge reads exactly the step's 10 or the
wide's 15, this stage prices its offsets in whole steps
only, and the ledger must say so in those words — an
interior landing (11..=14) is the first mid-step price
this stage has ever read, and a cliff landing (a price
at or past waning-750's own reading, in the direction
of the cave's 16) would mean the fall between the step
and the wide offset is not a turn on either stage. A
collapse, a step, a tax, or a cliff — the edge is
reported as found.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by
test — an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a
claim. (Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets
are as declared, expiry fires in both pools in each
edge room, and no dawn tax fires there.)
