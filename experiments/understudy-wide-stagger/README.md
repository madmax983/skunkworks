## The Principal and the Role

`wide-stagger-moths` took up `mirrored-moths`' closing seed
verbatim — *a stagger wider than the claim-step (~450+
ticks): does the mirror gap widen, hold at 2, or close when
the offset outlasts a whole journey leg?* — and answered it
in the FDTD cave, caution booked by source on a
dawn-crossing global clock. Both pools expire at lifetime
750; the one offset was widened from +375 to **+750** ticks,
a full lifetime, in three rooms: **staggered-wide** (taught
deadline +750) fed 54, burns **12**, checks 110, events 644
(202 irregular / 442 steady), learners 18, acquittals 145,
expired taught **31** / earned **20**; **mirrored-wide**
(earned deadline +750) fed 46, burns **12**, checks 125,
events 779 (251 / 528), learners 17, acquittals 166, expired
**89** / **17**; **coincident-wide** (the control — both
deadlines +750, coincident at the longest deadline 2250)
fed 43, burns **12**, checks 151, events 943 (342 / 601),
learners 18, acquittals 222, expired **42** / **20**. Five
of six predictions held; **P4 cracked** — neither wide
stagger beats its coincident control, because all three
rooms burn **12**: at the wide offset the cave reads three
deadline arrangements as one room. The mirror gap
**closed** (2 at +375, 0 at +750), the waiting apart fell
from 4 burns to 0, and widening taxed the staggers toward
the control (9 → 12 taught-side, 11 → 12 earned-side) while
the coincident line barely moved (13 → 12). The seven-room
cave ordering: **staggered 9 < mirrored 11 < staggered-wide
12 = mirrored-wide 12 = coincident-wide 12 <
staggered-both 13 < waning-750 16** — a stagger only exists
while the offset fits inside a journey.

The role, extracted precisely: *the both-pools waning room
at one lifetime, re-performed with the stagger widened to a
full lifetime in both directions beside a coincident
control at the same longest deadline — priced on fed,
burns, checks, events in both kinds, first learners,
acquittals, and the two expiry counts — and on whether the
principal's wide dissolution (three arrangements, one
price) survives the swap of machinery, on a stage whose own
resolution limit is the very quantity the seed names.*

## The Different Machinery

The claim-queue stage of `understudy-mirrored`: no field,
no grid, no positions, no gradient, no flight. Each moth
owns a claim queue (wait 300–500 ticks; the next claim is a
coin flip — both lamps emit at mean period 16, and without
a field brightness has no channel); cautious moths sample
the lamp's next 5 intervals directly (cost ≤ 96 ticks) and
judge CV > 0.35; messages travel on a bus at fixed delivery
probabilities (burns 0.05, refusals and packets 0.30).
Caution is booked by source in kind: caution from a moth's
own burns and from delivered burn messages is **earned**;
caution from re-judging an evidence packet is **taught**
(an acquittal spends taught first, popping that unit's gain
tick with it — if no taught unit remains, the oldest earned
unit is spent, exactly as the principal books it).

Twenty-four twin rooms, same seed. The first twenty-one
are the inherited queue script unchanged (ledgers pinned
exact by tests — including this stage's staggered fed 54 /
burns 11, expired 48 taught / 15 earned; staggered-both fed
54 / burns 11, expired 48 / 10; mirrored fed 55 / burns 12,
expired 62 / 11). The three new rooms run this stage's
waning-750 room **verbatim**, differing only in the offset,
widened from +375 to +750 owner-ticks:

- **StaggeredWide** — taught units expire at gain + 750 +
  750 owner-ticks; earned units at gain + 750.
- **MirroredWide** — earned units expire at gain + 750 +
  750 owner-ticks; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror,
  at the wide offset.
- **CoincidentWide** — BOTH deadlines offset +750, so the
  deadlines stay coincident at the longest deadline (2250
  owner-ticks); only duration changes. The control, as the
  principal planted it.

The stage translation, named in the open: the principal's
clock is the cave's shared tick, struck continuously. A
queue is sequential, so each unit here carries its gain
tick on its **owner's personal clock** — the ticks that
moth's own turns have consumed, running across dawns and
never reset — and expiry is discovered only when its owner
next moves: at the top of its turn's claims, and at each
dawn on its personal clock. There is no dawn tax in these
rooms. No RNG is drawn by expiry.

One stage fact shapes the whole performance, and it was
known before the curtain: this stage's time moves in
**claim-sized steps of roughly 450 owner-ticks** — a unit's
age is observed only at claim tops — and both earlier
stagger performances **collapsed** for exactly that reason.
At +375, staggered read its coincident control's headline
entire (fed 54, burns 11, checks 100, events 695, expired
taught 48 in both) and differed only in the earned census
(15 against 10); mirrored collapsed onto **waning-750
itself** (fed 55, burns 12, checks 97, events 671 exact),
differing only in the earned census (11 against 16) and a
single acquittal. The +750 offset is the first stagger this
stage can in principle **resolve**: it spans more than one
claim-step. So the wide role arrives here with a sharper
question than the principal asked the cave. In the cave,
three arrangements read as one room *despite* a clock that
sees every tick — the dissolution was the law. On this
stage, a one-room reading at +375 proved nothing about the
law, only about the stage's eyes. At +750 the eyes suffice:
if the three wide rooms still read as one, the principal's
dissolution is performed entire, on machinery that could
have told them apart; if they separate, the separation
marks the exact step-width where this stage starts to see
staggers at all — and the cave's equality becomes a fact
about journeys, not about clocks.

## Falsification Condition

The principal's claim — *past a journey leg, a stagger
dissolves: the mirror gap and the coincidence gap fall to
zero together, and three arrangements of the same deadlines
burn as one room* — is falsified on this stage room by
room: a wide room in which no packet is ever witnessed, or
in which either pool never expires, tests nothing; if
coincident-wide burns **below** either wide stagger,
coincident deadlines at the longest deadline beat a stagger
the offset outlasts a leg, and the dissolution is acquitted
at the exact comparison built to indict it; if the wide
mirror gap lands far from the wide taught stagger (outside
the declared band of 3), the gap did not close here in any
direction the cave would recognize; and if the three wide
rooms separate cleanly in the headline — staggered-wide,
mirrored-wide, coincident-wide each at its own burns — the
cave's one-room reading was a property of the cave's
journeys, not of the widening itself, and the ledger must
say so in those words. A collapse at +750, on a stage that
can see +750, is the law performed; a collapse at +375
never was.

The burns comparisons are reported, not asserted by test —
an understudy does not edit the ledger to save a claim.
(Instrument facts *are* asserted: the offsets are as
declared, expiry fires in both pools in each wide room,
and no dawn tax fires there.)

Read the two orderings against each other and the

ended before the deadlines could separate; on the queue
stage, widening separates exactly one thing — the pool the
room spends first. A stagger wider than the journey is a
longer deadline wearing the stagger's coat in the cave;
here it is a tax on the pool that pays for everything, and
a census entry on the pool that pays for almost nothing.
Which forgetting waits is worth −3 burns at width on this

to the machinery, and only the asymmetry survives the
