offset the staggered arrangement *is* the waning-750 room (both
pools at gain + 750, burns **16**); at +375 the taught stagger
reads the shelf entire (**9**). Somewhere in (0, 375] the taught
price falls from 16 to 9, and nobody had ever priced the leg's
interior. This planting keeps the evidence room's packets,
re-judgement, aversion and acquittal rule verbatim, books caution
by source exactly as `firstedge-moths` does, expires **both**
pools at lifetime **750**, and sets the one offset to **+150**
or **+225** — in three rooms at each address:

- **Staggered-150 / -225** — taught units expire at gain + 750 +
  offset; earned units at gain + 750.
- **Mirrored-150 / -225** — earned units expire at gain + 750 +
  offset; taught units at gain + 750. The mirror, in the west.
- **Coincident-150 / -225 (the controls)** — *both* pools expire
  at gain + 750 + offset. The deadlines stay coincident with each
  other; only the duration is longer (longest deadlines 900 /
  975). At zero offset the coincident control *is* waning-750
  (16); at +375 the coincident-both room reads 13.

One hundred and one twin rooms, same seed: the ninety-five
parents, unchanged (tests pin their ledgers exactly — including
staggered-471 fed 50 / burns 10, the split itself), plus the six
western rooms. The moths know only what the field delivers and
