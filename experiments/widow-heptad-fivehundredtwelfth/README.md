## Falsification Condition

The claim — *at the tower's hour, the row reads 37 / 59 / 68 /
51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 25 / 36 / 47 / 13 at 512th grain: the 512th
stands above the 256th (a third rise, by exactly eleven
again), and its count (94) stands exactly at the 256th's own*
— is falsified if:

- any cell reports TRUE LOCK 7:5 — then the pair could hold
  after all, at an address every earlier grid skipped;
- any of the ten known cells fails to reproduce its ledger —
  then the instruments are not counting the same pair, and
  the shape is a counting difference, not a discovery;
- (−1.001953125, 12) recounts at or below the rise threshold
  42 — then the remainder holds, halves, or falls, and the
  third rise falls;
- (−1.001953125, 12) recounts with perfect other than 94 —
  then the count's stand at the top edge is a counting error,
  not a finding.

All pinned by unit tests (6/6), including the full measured
row — 37 / 59 / 68 / 51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 25 / 36 / 47 / 13, the
512th's perfect 94, and the SLOPE RISES A THIRD TIME AT 512TH
GRAIN verdict itself — so the record cannot quietly heal
itself into a slope.
