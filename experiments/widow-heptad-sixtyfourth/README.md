## Concept

The pair is unchanged, and so is the counting. Two neurons joined
by exactly one synapse:

- **The driver** — Regular Spiking, constant current I=12. Solo
  measured period: **39.60 ticks** (568 spikes in the window).
- **The follower** — Fast Spiking, constant current I=4.8. Solo
  measured period: **28.01 ticks** (803 spikes). Seven follower
  beats span **196.08** against five driver cycles' **198.00** —
  near 7:5, the follower **early by 1.92 ticks per super-cycle**.

A **super-cycle** is five consecutive driver cycles. It is
**perfect** when it holds exactly seven follower beats AND each
beat's phase lies within ±0.06 of that beat's median phase for
the regime (the two-gate rule, carried over unchanged). An
**episode** is a maximal run of consecutive perfect super-cycles.
Each cell is counted over **24,000 ticks** (first 1,500
discarded; 112 super-cycles), beside the uncoupled pair on the
same window (83/112 perfect, longest 17).

The survey is the row entire as thirtysecond left it — seven
known cells, the five dose anchors plus cliff's ledge and
thirtysecond's tread, both now anchors in their own right —
plus the one sixty-fourth step between the tread and the foot:
**−1.015625 at delay 12**, the exact midpoint of the second
thirty-second, the only address inside it no instrument had
priced. The shape lines are carried unchanged: plateau line
**55**, isolation line **34**. Two further lines price the

## Falsification Condition

The claim — *at the tower's hour, the row reads 37 / 59 / 68 /
51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 13 at sixty-fourth grain: the sixty-fourth
stands above the tread-hold band entire, a second ledge in the
slope's lower half, and its count (93) stands above the tread's
(88)* — is falsified if:

- any cell reports TRUE LOCK 7:5 — then the pair could hold
  after all, at an address every earlier grid skipped;
- any of the seven known cells fails to reproduce its ledger —
  then the instruments are not counting the same pair, and the
  shape is a counting difference, not a discovery;
- (−1.015625, 12) recounts at or below 42 — then it stands in
  the tread-hold band or below, the rise falls, and the lower
  half is a hold, a halving, or a whole fall after all;
- (−1.015625, 12) recounts with perfect at or below the
  tread's 88 — then the counts did descend at this grain and
  the second ledge is a run-only tooth like its elder.

All pinned by unit tests (6/6), including the full measured
row — 37 / 59 / 68 / 51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 13, the sixty-fourth's
perfect 93, and the SLOPE RISES verdict itself — so the record
cannot quietly heal itself into a slope.
