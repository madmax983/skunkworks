## Falsification Condition

The claim — *at the tower's hour, the row reads 37 / 59 / 68 /
51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 25 / 36 / 47 / 21 / 35 / 13 at 2048th
grain: the 2048th stands above the 1024th entire (the fall
reverses at the last grain), and its count (90) stands above
the foot's own 74* — is falsified if:

- any cell reports TRUE LOCK 7:5 — then the pair could hold
  after all, at an address every earlier grid skipped;
- any of the twelve known cells fails to reproduce its
  ledger — then the instruments are not counting the same
  pair, and the shape is a counting difference, not a
  discovery;
- (−1.00048828125, 12) recounts at or below the hold line
  18 — then the reversal is a counting error, and the fall
  kept its halving after all;
- (−1.00048828125, 12) recounts with perfect other than
  90 — then the count's overshoot of the foot is a counting
  error, not a finding.

All pinned by unit tests (6/6), including the full measured
row — 37 / 59 / 68 / 51 / 45 / 36 / 46 / 25 / 36 / 47 / 21 /
35 / 13, the 2048th's perfect 90, and the FALL REVERSES AT
2048TH GRAIN verdict itself — so the record cannot quietly
heal itself into a halving.
