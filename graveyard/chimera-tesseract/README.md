# Chimera Tesseract 🧬🧊

**Parents:** `experiments/tesseract-ops` × `experiments/chimera-lang`

## Concept
A visualization of a **4-Dimensional Hypercube** (Tesseract) where each of the 16 vertices is inhabited by a living **ChimeraVM** agent.

## Novel Trait: 4D Biological Topology
The agents live in a 4D space. The "breathing" of the tesseract (scaling in the W-axis) represents the metabolic rhythm of the colony. The rotation speed of the hypercube is driven by the total energy of the population.

## Lineage
- **Structure:** Inherits the `Vec4` mathematics and projection logic from `tesseract-ops`.
- **Biology:** Inherits the `ChimeraVM` and genetic execution from `chimera-lang`.
- **Emergence:** The visual stability of the 4D object is directly tied to the biological stability of the agents. If they die, the tesseract stops rotating and shrinks.

## Controls
- **Arrows / WASD**: Rotate the 3D camera around the 4D projection.
- **Visuals**:
  - **Sphere Size**: Agent Energy.
  - **Sphere Color**: Agent Metabolic State (Green = High Energy, Red = Low).
  - **Rotation Speed**: Total Colony Energy.
