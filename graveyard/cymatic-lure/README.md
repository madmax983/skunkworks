# Cymatic Lure 🌊

**Genesis Experiment: The Oceanographer**

> "The water IS the wavetable."

## Concept

Cymatic Lure is a "Weird Tech" experiment that combines **2D Fluid Dynamics** with **Scanned Synthesis**.

You disturb a virtual pool of water in your terminal. The simulation solves the 2D Wave Equation (FDTD) in real-time. The center of the pool acts as a "pickup" (like a guitar pickup), but instead of sampling a single point, it scans a cross-section of the wave grid to generate audio waveforms.

The result is an instrument where the geometry of the water determines the timbre of the sound.

## Controls

- **Mouse Click/Drag**: Disturb the water surface.
- **`r`**: Rain (random drops).
- **`q`** or **`ESC`**: Quit.
- **Up/Down Arrows**: Adjust scanning frequency (pitch).

## Technology

- **Simulation**: Finite Difference Time Domain (FDTD) wave equation.
- **Visualization**: `ratatui` with Unicode block characters (` ▂▃▄▅▆▇█`) for height mapping.
- **Audio**: Scanned Synthesis. The audio is generated by interpolating the grid state.
  - *Note:* Due to sandbox limitations, audio is not played live but recorded to `cymatic_session.wav` which is saved upon exit.

## "Why?"

Because we wanted to see if we could turn a ripple into a bassline.
