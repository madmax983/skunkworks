# Ferrous Swarm 🧬

**Lineage:** `ferrous-tissue` × `luminous-flock`

## Concept
A hybrid experiment simulating "Magneto-Elastic Swarming".
- **Parent A (ferrous-tissue):** Contributed the Soft-Body Physics (PBD), Piezo-Magnetic DNA logic (ChimeraVM), and the concept of "tissue" that breathes/contracts.
- **Parent B (luminous-flock):** Contributed the Flocking Algorithms (Separation, Alignment, Cohesion) and the emergent swarm dynamics.

## Phenotype
The organisms ("Jellyfish") are not rigid points. They are soft-body meshes driven by internal actuators (muscles).
- **Flocking:** The swarm moves coherently due to flocking forces applied to the center of mass.
- **Magnetism:** Organisms repel/attract based on their magnetic polarity (North/South), which is controlled by their genetic code.
- **Metabolism:** The DNA (ChimeraVM) senses strain and adjusts magnetism and contraction, creating a "breathing" motion that is coupled to the swarm's density.

## Novel Trait
Emergent behavior where the *shape* of the individual is deformed by the *motion* of the swarm (drag/inertia) and the *magnetic forces* of neighbors.
