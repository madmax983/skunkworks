# gray-chimera 🧬

**Lineage:** `crates/gray-scott` × `experiments/chimera-lang`

## Concept
"Morphogenetic Genetics"

Agents equipped with `ChimeraVM` brains are dropped into a toxic `gray-scott` reaction-diffusion chemical substrate. The agents act as evolutionary nodes that must navigate the Turing patterns.

## Novel Trait
The `V` chemical (predator) damages the agents, while the `U` chemical (prey) heals them. By reading the local chemical concentrations as sensory input, the agents push intent to the VM, evolving their DNA to optimize their pathing. As they navigate, they also leak `V` chemical, altering the reaction-diffusion patterns themselves and creating a macroscopic-microscopic feedback loop.
