**Terminal Redundancy / Technological Regression**

- **Redundancy**: The `experiments/` directory already hosts `luminous-flock` (TUI flocking) and `fluid-rain` (executed for O(N^2) SPH).
- **Regression**: Violation of ADR 045 (Compute Shaders preferred for fluid simulation).

## Comparison
- Inferior to `luminous-flock` in emergent behavior.
- Identical regression traits to the executed `fluid-rain` specimen.

## Verdict
**CONDEMN**
Grace Period: 24 hours.
Salvation requires:
1. Implementation of spatial partitioning (Grid/Quadtree) to reduce complexity.
2. OR Migration to GPU Compute Shaders.
