# Hyper Fold 🧲🦢🧊

**Status**: Experimental
**Lineage**: `hyper-tissue` × `ferrous-fold`

A 4D "Hyper-Origami" simulation where a Miura-ori sheet exists in 4D space and folds into the 4th dimension based on "Hyper-Magnetic" forces controlled by ChimeraVM agents.

## Concept

This experiment simulates a "Hyper-Crumple":
- **4D Mesh**: A Miura-ori grid generated by `origami` is embedded in 4D space (W=0 initially).
- **Hyper-Magnetism**: Each vertex has a 4D magnetic polarity (`Vec4`). Parallel polarities attract, anti-parallel repel.
- **Chimera Logic**: Agents inhabit each vertex. They sense local strain and neighbors' W-position, and modify their own `magnetic_polarity.w` to fold the sheet "out" of the 3D universe.
- **Emergence**: The sheet should form impossible 3D projections as it folds through hyperspace.

## Controls

- **Left Click + Drag**: Rotate the 3D projection camera.
- **Space**: Reset Simulation.
- **R**: Randomize Polarities.

## Implementation Details

- **Physics**: Uses `PbdSystem4D` (ported from `hyper-tissue`) with added 4D Magnetic Forces.
- **Rendering**: Stereographic projection of 4D points to 3D, rendered via `macroquad`.
- **Genetics**: ChimeraVM agents evolve folding strategies.

## Lineage

- **Parent A**: `hyper-tissue` (4D Soft Body Physics).
- **Parent B**: `ferrous-fold` (Magnetic Origami).
- **Novel Trait**: 4D Origami. Folding across the W-axis.
