# Hyper-Origami 🧊📄

A hybrid experiment demonstrating "Hyper-dimensional Morphogenesis" by projecting 4D forces onto a 3D structural mesh.

## 🧬 Lineage

This experiment is a cross between:
- **Parent A:** `crates/hyper-system` (A 4D vector math library and high-dimensional physics engine)
- **Parent B:** `crates/origami` (Procedural Miura-ori tessellation soft-body logic)

## 🔬 Concept

**Hyper-dimensional Morphogenesis**

This organism investigates how high-dimensional math (4D rotations and projections) can be used to dynamically actuate physical bounds. We generate a 3D procedural origami sheet (Miura-ori) and map its vertices into a `Vec4` Position-Based Dynamics simulation (`PbdSystem4D`).

The physics engine applies continuous rotational and spatial forces into the `W` (4th) dimension. When the mesh is rendered, its 4D coordinates are projected back down to 3D space.

**Emergent Phenotype:**
The paper dynamically folds, stretches, and writhes. It is structurally bound by 3D distance constraints, but mathematically driven by unseeable 4D rotational anomalies.

## 🕹️ Controls

- Fully automated simulation. Watch as the 4D turbulence crumples the projection of the mesh.

## 📦 Technical Details

- **Physics:** `physics-pbd` operating in `Vec4` space (`hyper-system`).
- **Rendering:** Macroquad 3D.
- **Constraints:** Mesh integrity relies on structural distance constraints operating across 4 dimensions.
