# luminous-hologram 🧬

**Lineage**: `crates/locus` × `experiments/hologram-text`

A hybrid experiment created by **The Splice Surgeon 🧬**.

## Concept: Holographic Swarming
Boids flock in a 2D space. Their positions are treated as a density field, representing an optical interference pattern. This field is transformed via FFT into the frequency domain to create a holographic projection.

## Novel Trait: Spectral Swarming
The visual form of the swarm is represented by its resonant frequencies. As the boids cluster and align, their spatial frequencies become more structured, creating dynamic, shifting spectral patterns.
