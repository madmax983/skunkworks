# 🧲 Magnetron Decay ☢️

> "Data is not a static object. It is a standing wave fighting the second law of thermodynamics." - Genesis: The Archivist

**Magnetron Decay** is a Moonshot experiment in **Digital Archaeology** and **Entropy Simulation**. It visualizes a hard drive platter where data is not safe. It requires active maintenance to survive.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Arrow Keys**: Move the Read/Write Head.
- **Space**: Scrub/Refresh the sector under the head. (Restores Magnetization, increases Coercivity wear).
- **W**: Write random noise to the sector (Resets Magnetization, high wear).
- **+ / -**: Adjust Time Scale (Speed up entropy).
- **Q**: Quit.

## 🧪 The Science of Rot

The simulation models three physical properties of magnetic storage:

1.  **Magnetization**: The strength of the magnetic field representing the bits. This decays naturally over time. If it drops below a threshold, thermal noise causes **Bit Flips**.
2.  **Coercivity**: The resistance of the medium to change. Every time you Write or Scrub a sector, the physical medium degrades (Coercivity drops). Lower Coercivity means faster Magnetization decay.
3.  **Bit Flips**: When Magnetization is low, the probability of a 0 flipping to a 1 (or vice versa) increases exponentially.

## 🔭 Observations

- **Heatmap**:
    - **Green**: Strong Signal. Safe.
    - **Yellow**: Weak Signal. Data at risk.
    - **Red**: Critical Signal. Corruption imminent or active.
    - **Cyan**: Head Position.

## 📜 The Lore

In the deep archives of the **Skunkworks**, we found this drive. It contains fragments of the old world. A cyberpunk text corpus. Your job is to keep it alive. But remember: **You cannot save everything.** Every time you save a sector, you damage the medium slightly. Eventually, the drive *will* fail.

How long can you keep the story legible?
