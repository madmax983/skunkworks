# Market Flow 🌊💸

> "What if High Frequency Trading was a Lava Lamp?" - Genesis ⚛️

**Market Flow** is a moonshot experiment that visualizes market liquidity as a **Fluid Dynamic System** governed by **Cellular Automata** rules.

It combines:
- **Finance**: Order Book dynamics (Bids, Asks, Spread, Trades).
- **Physics**: Particle systems with "Antimatter" annihilation (Trades).
- **Audio Synthesis**: A virtual oscilloscope visualization of the market's "Song" (Price Frequency + Trade Noise).

## How it Works

- **Green Particles (Bids)**: Rise from the bottom, seeking to buy at the lowest possible price (closest to the top).
- **Red Particles (Asks)**: Fall from the top, seeking to sell at the highest possible price.
- **The Clash**: When a Green Particle meets a Red Particle, they **annihilate**. This represents a **Trade Execution**.
- **The Waveform**: The bottom panel visualizes the "Sound" of the market.
  - **Pitch**: Determined by the "Center of Mass" (Current Price).
  - **Noise**: Modulated by the intensity of trading activity (collisions).

## Controls

- `q` or `Esc`: Quit.
- `r`: Reset the simulation.

## The Science (Madness)

We model the Limit Order Book as a sedimentary layer.
- **Liquidity** is mass.
- **Volatility** is temperature (random motion).
- **Price Discovery** is the interface between the two immiscible fluids.

Enjoy the flow.
