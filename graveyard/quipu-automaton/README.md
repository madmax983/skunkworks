# Quipu Automaton 🧶⚙️

> "The knotted cord is the program. The wheel is the processor."

A hybrid experiment combining **Inca Quipu** data structures with a **Mechanical Differential Analyzer**.

## 🧬 Lineage

- **Parent A:** `experiments/harmonic-engine` (Physics-based Mechanical Computing)
- **Parent B:** `experiments/quipu-symphony` (Inca Data Structures & Visuals)
- **Genetic Engineer:** The Splice Surgeon

## 🔬 Concept

This simulation visualizes a mechanical computer that uses knotted cords as both input and output media.
- **Input:** A Quipu cord hangs down, feeding into the machine.
- **Processing:** A "Sensor" lever feels the knots as they pass. The displacement drives a mechanical integrator (simulated with `rapier2d`).
- **Output:** The result of the integration accumulates and is tied into a new Output Cord.

## 🕹️ Controls

- **Space**: Pause/Resume simulation.
- **R**: Reset simulation.
- **Q**: Quit.

## 🧠 The "Why"

Computing history often overlooks non-Western traditions. The Quipu was a powerful database and accounting system. This experiment imagines a timeline where the Incas developed mechanical automation for their textile-based computing.
